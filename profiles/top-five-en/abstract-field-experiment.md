Matching Grants for Savings: Evidence from a Field Experiment
We randomize matching rates for deposits into formal savings accounts among 9,420 households in 212 villages of Karsten Province. Matching rates of 25, 50, and 100 percent raise the probability of opening an account by 9, 17, and 31 percentage points relative to a 0 percent match. Deposits rise with the matching rate at an elasticity of 0.62 in the lowest wealth tercile and 0.21 in the highest tercile. A mechanism-design component elicits each household's willingness to pay for the match, and households in the top quartile of willingness to pay save 2.4 times more per kora of subsidy than the average household. A targeting rule based on elicited valuations raises total deposits per kora of subsidy by 38 percent.
