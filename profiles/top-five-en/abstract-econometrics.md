Inference with Staggered Adoption under Cluster Dependence
Researchers estimate treatment effects from panels in which units adopt a policy at staggered dates. We derive the asymptotic distribution of the two-way fixed-effects estimator when adoption dates are assigned at the cluster level and outcomes correlate within clusters. The estimator converges to a weighted average of cohort-time effects whose weights sum to one and turn negative for 23 percent of cohort-time cells in a panel of 3,100 firms. We propose an imputation estimator with nonnegative weights by construction, and we prove that its cluster-robust variance estimator is consistent as the number of clusters grows. Simulations calibrated to 14 benchmark panels show that the proposed confidence intervals cover the true effect at a rate of 94.8 percent.
