Public Ratings with Heterogeneous Buyers
A platform publishes a rating of an unknown state, and a continuum of buyers with privately known thresholds each decide whether to buy. We solve for the sender's optimal rating under any distribution of thresholds. The optimal rating induces two posteriors, and each posterior is a tangency point of the threshold distribution with its concave envelope. The value of the optimal rating equals the concave envelope evaluated at the prior. Full disclosure is optimal at every prior if and only if the threshold distribution lies below the 45-degree line, and an uninformative rating is optimal at every prior if and only if the distribution is concave. A rating with two grades attains the value of any finer rating. We apply the results to a platform that certifies the quality of rental listings.
