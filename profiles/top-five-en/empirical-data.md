II. Data
A. Sources
We draw on three administrative sources. The MSIA registry reports monthly spells of employment, pension receipt, DI receipt, and UI receipt for every resident aged 18 to 70 from 2010 through 2022, together with birth date, sex, education, and region. The Marden Revenue Service employer-employee panel reports annual earnings for every job and links each job to a firm identifier. The Revenue Service corporate files report annual sales, payroll, and headcount for each of 412,000 firms. We link the sources through a scrambled national identifier with a match rate of 99.6 percent.
B. Samples and Variables
The worker sample contains every resident born between July 1, 1956 and June 30, 1959, a total of 452,310 persons. The firm sample contains the 187,400 firms with 10 or more employees in 2017. We measure outcomes of workers at ages 60 to 62. A worker is employed in a calendar year if the Revenue Service reports positive earnings and the MSIA registry reports six or more months of employment. A hire is a worker whose first employment spell at the firm begins in the calendar year, and we split hires into three age groups: under 30, 30 to 49, and 50 and above. A retained worker is an employee aged 60 to 62 who holds a job at the firm in the calendar year. We express hires and retained workers per 100 employees of the firm in the previous year.
C. Summary Statistics
Table 1 reports summary statistics. Panel A reports worker characteristics by side of the cutoff, measured at age 58 before any eligibility age applies. The two groups match on sex, education, region, employment, and earnings. Panel B reports firm characteristics in 2017. The median firm employs 31 workers, the mean firm employs 87.4 workers, and the ladder of the median firm has 5 rungs. Workers aged 60 to 62 make up 4.1 percent of employment in the mean firm, and workers born in the 24 months after the cutoff make up 6.7 percent.
