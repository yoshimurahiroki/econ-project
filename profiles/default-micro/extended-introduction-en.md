Private activation increases late deliveries by 3.6 percentage points from a 7 percent control mean. Implementation rates among linked plants are 34 and 76 percent under private and joint activation; rates among stand-alone plants are 79 and 81 percent.

Operating records: plants sharing a feeder wash, cook and chill batches and transfer intermediate food within each estate. Prices are higher from 7 a.m. to 10 a.m.; movable batch starts have 30–90 minutes of flexibility. Recommendations minimize the highest combined 15-minute load over feasible schedules. Joint activation implements sets with complete agreement and leaves remaining sets on their existing schedules. Tariff discounts and interfirm transfers equal zero.
