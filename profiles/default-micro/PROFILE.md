# Default economics writing profile

Profile ID: default-micro. Languages: English and Japanese. Apply [PROSE](../../runtime/PROSE.md) throughout the principles, source cards, worked passages and explanations.

## Principles

Apply [PROSE's composition, audience and language rules](../../runtime/PROSE.md#select-the-profile). Build a connected spoken explanation before shaping academic wording. Preserve the manuscript's effective vocabulary, notation and rhythm. Choose the study-specific relation and facts that advance its answer.

Worked passages use their own source cards and independently authored prose. Select an example only for a remaining language or argument choice; retain the current study's population, mechanism, quantities and result. [Profile authoring](../../skills/econ-writing/references/profile.md) owns construction of new profiles and worked examples.

For chapter-length composition, read the corresponding complete chapter in the [English subscription study](subscription-study-en.md) or [Japanese subscription study](subscription-study-ja.md). They develop the question, customer choice, allocation, experimental method and results as connected sections. Match a requested new example to its model paper's corresponding section length and argumentative development; keep short examples for local wording choices. Carry an established actor or quantity into the next sentence, then state the new relation. Before: “支援は有用である。また、予算がある。” After: “支援は顧客の継続確率を高める。企業は、その増分利益を予算の範囲で最大にする支援先を選ぶ。”

## Select the passage function

Read these principles once while their full text remains in the active context. Resolve a remaining writing choice through the example matching its language and function. Each stable example ID has its own registered section. Apply the operation to the current passage's facts and logical dependencies. A resolved writing choice advances to the finished artifact.

The examples use fictional settings and quantities. Their input cards establish every empirical value and completed revision in the example. The Cournot and subsidy cases supply equations for direct verification. Select one needed case within its registered section and keep the current study's own population, mechanism and result. Choose paragraph length, order and rhythm from the current argument and audience.
