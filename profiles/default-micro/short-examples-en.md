# Short functional examples: English

Profile: default-micro. These fictional source cards and worked passages apply the [profile principles](PROFILE.md#principles). Select the example resolving the current writing choice.

## Conclusion and comparison

Example ID: default-micro.en.conclusion.

**Input:**
Choose one service to maximize annual net benefit. The shuttle's benefit is £84,000 and its cost is £60,000. The bicycle service's net benefit is £19,000. Each option fits the £65,000 budget. One implementation slot is available.

**Ideal output:**
Choose the shuttle service. Its annual benefit of £84,000 less its cost of £60,000 gives a net benefit of £24,000. The gain over the bicycle service's £19,000 net benefit is £5,000. Both options fit the £65,000 budget, so allocate the implementation slot to the service with the higher net benefit.

**Generalizable operation:**
Lead with the choice, calculate net benefits in the same period and currency, and state the difference determining the allocation among feasible options.

## Institution and mechanism

Example ID: default-micro.en.mechanism.

**Input:**
A cooperative introduced morning and afternoon reservations for a shared mill. Farmers whose own vehicles are free in the afternoon use those vehicles to carry grain. Hired transport carries a fee. Explain the relation between reservation time and transport cost.

**Ideal output:**
Farmers whose own vehicles are free in the afternoon reserve the afternoon mill slot and carry their grain in those vehicles. Matching the reservation to the vehicle's availability saves the fee for hiring transport for that load.

**Generalizable operation:**
Connect the reservation, available vehicle and transport fee. Explain the farmer's timing choice through the payment that choice changes.

## Quantities and relationship

Example ID: default-micro.en.quantities.

**Input:**
Describe observational data from 160 repair shops in 2024. Shops with a certified-worker share 20 percentage points higher had average revenue per worker-hour €12 higher. Report the between-shop association.

**Ideal output:**
Among 160 repair shops surveyed in 2024, shops with a certified-worker share 20 percentage points higher had average revenue per worker-hour €12 higher.

**Generalizable operation:**
Place the observed population, period, comparison width and outcome unit in the relationship. Select a subject and verb expressing the between-shop association.

## Revision and translation

Example ID: default-micro.en.translation.

**Input:**
Translate this renewal rule into natural English: 各借主は、家賃を全額支払い、建物の使用許可が有効である場合に、賃貸借を1年間更新できる。 Full payment and a valid occupancy permit jointly establish the right to renew.

**Ideal output:**
Each tenant has the right to renew the lease for one year after full rent payment while the building's occupancy permit is valid.

**Generalizable operation:**
Rebuild the sentence around the tenant's right, the two required conditions and the one-year renewal period.

## Abstract and Introduction

### Abstract

Example ID: default-micro.en.abstract.

**Input:**
A study randomly assigned 800 households in Alder Bay to an envelope group or a control group for a housing survey. The envelope group received a prepaid return envelope. Response rates were 64 percent in that group and 51 percent in the control group. Write the study's Abstract.

**Ideal output:**
Including a prepaid return envelope increased the housing survey response rate by 13 percentage points. We estimated this effect by randomly assigning 800 households in Alder Bay to an envelope group, with a 64 percent response rate, or a control group, with a 51 percent response rate.

**Generalizable operation:**
Lead with the main finding and give the group rates, population and assignment establishing it. Express the difference between the rates in percentage points.

### Introduction opening

Example ID: default-micro.en.introduction.

**Input:**
Write the opening of the same study's Introduction. Control households pay the postage to return the survey. Prepaid envelopes transfer that payment to the survey organizer. Random assignment among 800 households produced a 13-percentage-point difference in response rates.

**Ideal output:**
Including a prepaid return envelope increased the housing survey response rate by 13 percentage points. The survey organizer pays the return postage through the prepaid envelope, reducing the household's cost of replying. We estimated the effect of this change through random assignment among 800 households in Alder Bay.

**Generalizable operation:**
Connect the result to the institution changing household costs and the comparison measuring its effect. Explain the payment mechanism in the Introduction.

## Assignment and comparison

Example ID: default-micro.en.assignment.

**Input:**
An oversubscribed vocational training program allocates training opportunities by lottery. Explain a research design comparing subsequent paid employment among lottery winners and losers.

**Ideal output:**
We compare subsequent paid employment among applicants who won and lost the training lottery to estimate the effect of receiving an opportunity to attend vocational training.

**Generalizable operation:**
Connect the assignment mechanism to the outcome comparison and define the effect through the opportunity allocated by the lottery. Give the regression equation when it determines the requested interpretation of the estimate.
