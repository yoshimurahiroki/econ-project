# Lexical detection and integrated review

[PROSE](../runtime/PROSE.md#common-output) owns expression rules; CORE and EXECUTION govern the requested work and its actual operations.

The [dictionary](prose_rules.json) groups concrete Japanese and English phrasing by rhetorical function. The existing scanner preserves source coordinates across Unicode, markup and whitespace views. Matches locate passages for the same integrated review; a match is not a semantic verdict. Assess negation, scientific uncertainty, mathematical bounds, model conditions and source attribution through their actual propositions under PROSE.

Apply the numerical statistical reporting rule in PROSE.

Use `local_checks.py scan-text` to locate lexical candidates. Its default diagnostics return positions and counts without reproducing candidate text. The supported Stop hook requests a post-draft semantic review and correction of the complete final response. Review authored artifacts and the entire proposition as well; a lexical scan does not certify semantic compliance. Complete the reasoning or operation that produces the corrected artifact, then inspect the affected result.
