## Writing guidance

The prose rules synthesize the following writing guidance in the existing PROSE, econ-writing, default-micro profile and prose dictionary. The adopted principles govern content selection, argument structure and revision within the common writing process. The external skills remain at their sources.

- [Kiterlin, anti-defensive-writing](https://github.com/Kiterlin/anti-defensive-writing) and [Adkid-Zephyr, anti-defensive-writing](https://github.com/Adkid-Zephyr/anti-defensive-writing-Skill): organize a passage around its substantive claim, remove preemptive rebuttals and reconstruct defensive paragraphs as direct propositions.
- [msimchowitz, academic-voice](https://github.com/msimchowitz/writing-skills/blob/main/for-agents/academic-voice/SKILL.md): explain the next inference for an intelligent reader and preserve a precise scholarly register.
- [msimchowitz, writing-cadence](https://github.com/msimchowitz/writing-skills/blob/main/for-agents/writing-cadence/SKILL.md): revise rhythm at paragraph scale, repair choppiness and long subject–verb gaps, and let the argument determine sentence variation.
- [msimchowitz, better-usage](https://github.com/msimchowitz/writing-skills/blob/main/for-agents/better-usage/SKILL.md): align subjects and verbs with the actual actor or relation and restore a source-supported intermediate step when the sentence requires it.
- [k16shikano, Japanese technical-writing guidance](https://gist.github.com/k16shikano/fd287c3133457c4fd8f5601d34aa817d): select one central proposition per paragraph, introduce concepts in explanatory order and replace literal English metaphors with concrete Japanese operations.
- [academic-humanizer](https://github.com/AIScientists-Dev/academic-humanizer): preserve research claims, numerical results, equations and citations while revising scholarly exposition.
- [anti-ai-writing](https://github.com/cbsteh/anti-ai-writing): replace abstract significance and noun-heavy phrasing with substantive quantities, actors and relations.
- [natural-japanese](https://github.com/coji/natural-japanese): complete the explanation before compression, use natural Japanese connections and preserve the source's facts and register.
- [deslop-ja](https://github.com/nakashimatakaya/deslop-ja): revise structure and rhetorical stance together with wording; remove mechanical triples, uniform paragraphs and repetitive endings.
- [AnswerDotAI, write-prose](https://github.com/AnswerDotAI/skill-plugins/blob/main/plugins/codex-aai/skills/write-prose/SKILL.md): remove announcer labels, empty contrasts and repeated summaries; name actual actors and respect the reader's existing knowledge.
- [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing): use its concrete examples to identify inflated significance, superficial explanation, vague attribution, formulaic lists and decorative emphasis during editorial review.
- [Rapls, Japanese writing analysis](https://qiita.com/Rapls/items/b98b09ec57c7e4b7be05): allocate explanation by substantive importance and revise the writer's stance and paragraph progression before polishing vocabulary.
- [minorun365, Japanese writing guidance](https://qiita.com/minorun365/items/699e89544da8b0de300d): use clear subjects, one topic per block and restrained formatting while preserving the author's facts.
- [AIの力, Japanese writing rules](https://ai-no-chikara.com/posts/claude-skill-japanese-writing-rules): apply language principles from the start of drafting and adapt expression to the document's genre.
- [tabayashi, AI writing revision](https://zenn.dev/tabayashi/articles/how-to-deodorize-ai-writing): revise claims and actors before rhythm, words and symbols; judge the completed explanation as a whole.
- [coji, natural Japanese and rhythm](https://zenn.dev/coji/articles/natural-japanese-ai-smell-lint): let the content determine sentence and paragraph variation and retain substantive judgment beyond lexical matching.
- [TechnoEdge, Japanese writing guidance](https://www.techno-edge.net/article/2026/06/22/5209.html): prioritize the reader's understanding, concrete causal links and the information needed for the claim.
- [syun136_616, Japanese writing revision](https://qiita.com/syun136_616/items/bc64d7ab5cef6bae2aa7): replace impressive abstractions and boilerplate with sourced quantities, units and concrete actions.
