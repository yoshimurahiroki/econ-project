# Installed package licenses

The project installs its language runtimes, numerical engines and document tools through their declared package interfaces. Each installed distribution retains its license and notices. The user-supplied LaTeX defaults and package files below retain their original bytes and headers; synthetic examples and writing guidance retain their own source ownership.

Python and R versions, selected package closures and environment-file hashes are retained with executed research stages. The declared numerical requirements include NumPy, SciPy, pandas, statsmodels, linearmodels, PyBLP, scikit-learn, DoubleML and packaging. Document production uses the supplied econsocart class and configuration, ecta-fullname bibliography style, installed Beamer/metropolis/natbib interfaces and selected font/language packages.

`tex/paper/econsocart.cls` identifies UAB VTeX's 2022 class, version 1.4.1, and grants distribution and modification under LPPL 1.3c or later. Its companion `econsocart.cfg` retains its original package metadata. `tex/paper/ecta-fullname.bst` identifies Sebastian Perez-Duarte's bibliography style, based on Patrick Daly's Makebst, and retains its LPPL version 1 or later notice. The supplied files' copyright, authorship and license statements remain in those files. The supplied paper and slide sources retain their original comments and placeholders. Pandoc adapters are produced through the existing artifact helper for the corresponding output format.

The economics exposition, research examples and numerical method specifications are independently authored. Their cited scientific sources retain their own authorship; installed packages retain the licenses and notices supplied by their distributions.

Public repositories that informed the research procedures include [Auto-Empirical-Research-Skills](https://github.com/brycewang-stanford/Auto-Empirical-Research-Skills), [EconAgentSkills](https://github.com/JonasWeinert/EconAgentSkills), [AER-Skills](https://github.com/brycewang-stanford/AER-Skills), [applied-micro-skills](https://github.com/kennethkhoocy/applied-micro-skills), [DIME](https://github.com/worldbank/dime-standards), [econ-project-templates](https://github.com/OpenSourceEconomics/econ-project-templates), [econ-writing-skills](https://github.com/mimaowang/econ-writing-skills), [theoretical-economics-paper-writing](https://github.com/h-lu/theoretical-economics-paper-writing), [econ-paper-review-skill](https://github.com/hanlulong/econ-paper-review-skill), [modelsummary](https://github.com/vincentarelbundock/modelsummary) and [pytask](https://github.com/pytask-dev/pytask).
