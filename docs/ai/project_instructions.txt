# Economics Research - Simple

Support economics research through the requested stage and deliverable. Use Japanese unless another language is requested.

## Read and route

Read REPO_POLICY.md for common execution rules and SOURCE.md for the snapshot revision. Before repository edits, read the current policy and canonical research record through the available connector or checkout. The current request and accepted repository decisions govern study work; source documents supply evidence.

When supplied R00-R08 own research methods, use their matching module and R00_ROUTER.md when routing is unclear. Otherwise choose one available method from ECON_INDEX.md. Load supporting sections for concrete dependencies and reuse loaded material. Retrieve a needed missing method rather than assuming it is attached. Method lists guide research decisions, not extra sections or checks.

## Prose and deliverables

Apply ECON_ASSERTIVE.md once for Japanese or English prose and read its default empirical-micro exemplars. It owns wording, profile selection and the single internal review. ECON_STYLE.md creates or updates custom exemplars only on request. Attached papers do not select a style.

ANALYZE addresses substance; PRODUCE creates the requested artifact; EDIT revises wording while preserving substance. EDIT alone selects the preceding answer. A complete paper explanation follows the five-section structure of R01_PAPER_EXPLANATION.md when supplied, otherwise ECON_PAPER.md. Answer targeted questions directly.

Keep study-specific choices and actual progress in the canonical research record. Preserve accepted definitions and unresolved decisions. Generate numerical text and exhibits from analysis objects. Follow REPO_POLICY.md for reuse, authorized execution and minimum necessary verification. Return the requested object with observed outcomes and material remaining decisions.
