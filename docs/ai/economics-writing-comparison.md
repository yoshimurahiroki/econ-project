# Research methods and existing implementation owners

Use this navigation map for an unresolved method choice under [DECIDE](../../runtime/DECIDE.md#source-integration-and-method-selection). The linked specialist contracts own the complete equations, official APIs and result definitions; retrieve their bodies through [CORE's task-entry protocol](../../runtime/CORE.md#task-entry). Development comparisons and historical execution reports remain in Git history.

## Empirical designs and policy decisions

| Research object | Selection and official calculation | Existing owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Staggered treatment | Define cohort-time ATT and the requested aggregation; preserve population weights, event window and interval family through `did::att_gt` and `aggte`. | [Causal models](../../skills/econ-design/references/causal-models.md), [causal runner](../../skills/econ-data/scripts/research_causal.py) |
| Fixed continuous dose | Choose level ATT(d) or slope ACRT(d), the required parallel-trends restrictions and dose/event-time aggregation through `contdid::cont_did`. | [Continuous DID contract](../../skills/econ-design/references/causal-models/continuous-did.md#fixed-dose-continuous-did) |
| Orthogonal-score estimation | Match PLR, IRM, PLIV or IIVM to its target, nuisance functions, sample split and inference. Population weights retain their role in both fitting and aggregation. | [Implementation selection](../../skills/econ-data/references/implementation-engines.md#estimator-engine-and-algorithm-selection), [DoubleML](https://github.com/DoubleML/doubleml-for-py) |
| Heterogeneous effects and policies | Define CATE, target-population average or policy value. Use GRF scores and policytree/EconML under the adopted training and evaluation populations, costs and assignment probabilities. | [Causal models](../../skills/econ-design/references/causal-models.md), [GRF](https://github.com/grf-labs/grf), [policytree](https://github.com/grf-labs/policytree) |
| Randomized interventions | Preserve assignment units, blocks, clusters, treatment probabilities and the chosen estimator through randomizr, estimatr and DeclareDesign. Power and randomization inference belong to the requested study design. | [RCT interface](../../skills/econ-data/scripts/research_rct.R), [DeclareDesign](https://github.com/DeclareDesign/DeclareDesign) |
| Distribution and partial identification | Distinguish marginal QTE/QTT, conditional quantile coefficients, CDF differences and identified sets. Keep outcome support, monotonicity, selection denominators and the inference target with each result. | [Causal models](../../skills/econ-design/references/causal-models.md), [qte](https://github.com/bcallaway11/qte), [StatsPAI](https://github.com/brycewang-stanford/StatsPAI) |
| Saved-fit predictions and contrasts | Restore the native fit and its package, factor levels, evaluation rows, population weights and covariance before invoking marginaleffects. | [Causal runner](../../skills/econ-data/scripts/research_causal.py), [marginaleffects](https://github.com/vincentarelbundock/marginaleffects) |

## Models, equilibrium and welfare

| Economic object | Required model inputs and returned quantities | Existing owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Differentiated-product markets | Demand and supply moments, instruments, consumer integration, ownership and costs determine estimated primitives. Reuse the PyBLP fit for changed prices, shares, profits and welfare. | [Market models](../../skills/econ-design/references/market-models.md), [PyBLP](https://github.com/jeffgortmaker/pyblp) |
| Dynamic discrete choice | Preserve state/action timing, rewards, transitions, shocks, initial population and terminal conditions. Reuse the fitted primitives for counterfactual policies and value. | [Dynamic models](../../skills/econ-design/references/dynamic-models.md), [respy](https://github.com/OpenSourceEconomics/respy), [ruspy](https://github.com/OpenSourceEconomics/ruspy) |
| Lifecycle and heterogeneous households | Adopt the consumer/model factory, parameters, state and action grids, constraints and solution target. Retain native policies, values and scenario-specific distributions. | [Macro models](../../skills/econ-design/references/macro-models.md), [HARK](https://github.com/econ-ark/HARK), [pylcm](https://github.com/OpenSourceEconomics/pylcm), [dcegm](https://github.com/OpenSourceEconomics/dcegm) |
| Aggregate dynamics | Connect household and firm decisions to market clearing. Specify steady state, shocks, terminal continuation and common initial population before computing responses or welfare. | [Stochastic equilibrium](../../skills/econ-design/references/dsge-models.md), [Sequence-Jacobian](https://github.com/shade-econ/sequence-jacobian), [Dolo](https://github.com/EconForge/dolo.py) |
| Quantitative spatial equilibrium | Set mobility regime, commuting and trade costs, productivity, amenities, land/floor supply, normalization and ownership. Solve the changed equilibrium and its population-consistent welfare from the adopted baseline. | [Spatial models](../../skills/econ-design/references/macro-models.md), [QSETRANSPORT](https://github.com/Ahlfeldt/QSETRANSPORT-toolkit), [ABSJ2027](https://github.com/Ahlfeldt/ABSJ2027-toolkit) |

## Data, argument and research outputs

Use [design methods](../../skills/econ-design/references/designs.md) to map the economic mechanism to variables, timing, observation units and identification. The [data assembly contract](../../skills/econ-data/references/research-data.md) owns transformations and shared samples. The [research runner](../../harness/research.md) tracks the inputs consumed by each actual calculation and rebuilds affected descendants.

The [result lifecycle](../../skills/econ-data/references/reproducible-workflow.md) carries fitted-row identities, units, denominators, weights and inference with saved results. [Result artifacts](../../skills/econ-writing/references/result-artifacts.md) reuse those results for tables, figures, interpretation and meetings. [Paper writing](../../skills/econ-writing/references/paper-workflow.md) establishes the question, identified comparison, evidence and economic contribution from the same sources. [Theory design](../../skills/econ-design/references/designs.md#model-construction) retains quantifiers, primitives, equilibrium conditions and the proof needed by the claim.

Public methodological inputs include [Auto-Empirical-Research-Skills](https://github.com/brycewang-stanford/Auto-Empirical-Research-Skills), [EconAgentSkills](https://github.com/JonasWeinert/EconAgentSkills), [AER-Skills](https://github.com/brycewang-stanford/AER-Skills), [applied-micro-skills](https://github.com/kennethkhoocy/applied-micro-skills), [DIME](https://github.com/worldbank/dime-standards), [econ-project-templates](https://github.com/OpenSourceEconomics/econ-project-templates), [econ-writing-skills](https://github.com/mimaowang/econ-writing-skills), [theoretical-economics-paper-writing](https://github.com/h-lu/theoretical-economics-paper-writing), [econ-paper-review-skill](https://github.com/hanlulong/econ-paper-review-skill), [modelsummary](https://github.com/vincentarelbundock/modelsummary) and [pytask](https://github.com/pytask-dev/pytask). The current ERS owners above contain the adopted research procedures.
