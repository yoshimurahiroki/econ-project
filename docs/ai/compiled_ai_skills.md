# Research skills

## Common entry and deliverable execution

Read [econ-workflow](../../.agents/skills/econ-workflow/SKILL.md) once at the start of every new economics task. Its brief entry establishes the requested result, scope, authority, evidence and unresolved dependencies; it does not require a plan, record, delegation or review for a bounded task. Support calls and reference reads continue the same task without restarting entry. Reconsider routing only when the objective changes or a dependency remains unresolved. Select the executor by the requested deliverable. Read the support and references that establish its dependencies. For explanation and writing, select content by the economic question and contribution. Use specialist records to establish the science. An editorial review assesses content selection and expression. Assess design, estimation and inference when the user requests that scientific assessment.

## Composition, coordination and review ownership

Econ-writing composes complete papers through its [paper workflow](../../.agents/skills/econ-writing/references/paper-workflow.md) for requested paper or section drafts. Econ-workflow integrates interdependent research and selects methods for each dependency. Econ-review assesses the requested claim or artifact; its oversight reference supports an independent assessment or a contested execution finding. Apply [econ-assertive's common prose rule](../../.agents/skills/econ-assertive/SKILL.md#common-prose-rule) to every economics output alongside the selected research method.
