# Lexical detection and integrated review

[CORE](../runtime/CORE.md), [PROSE](../runtime/PROSE.md) and [EXECUTION](../runtime/EXECUTION.md) own the single integrated review of the requested result, its truth conditions, argument, expression and completed operations.

The [dictionary](prose_rules.json) groups concrete Japanese and English phrasing by rhetorical function. The existing scanner preserves source coordinates across Unicode, markup and whitespace views. Matches locate passages for the same integrated review; a match is not a semantic verdict. General negation, mathematical bounds, model conditions and source attribution are assessed through their actual propositions. Unmatched paraphrases retain the same prohibited function under PROSE.

Use `.agents/harness/local_checks.py scan-text` for a requested or consequential lexical check. Its default diagnostics return positions and counts without reproducing candidate text. Complete the reasoning or operation that produces the corrected artifact, then inspect the affected result. No per-pattern approval, individual meaning record or host-authenticated publication gate is required.
