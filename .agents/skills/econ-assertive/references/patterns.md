# A revision example

This fictional example concerns an oversubscribed apprenticeship program. No effect estimate is supplied.

Draft:

> Applicants enter a lottery. Some receive a training place. We observe later employment. We compare the groups.

Possible revision:

> We compare later employment among lottery winners and losers to estimate how an offer of apprenticeship training changes applicants' access to paid work.

Japanese:

> 定員を超える応募者の中から受講者を抽選で選ぶ制度を利用し、当選者と落選者のその後の就業を比較する。これにより、職業訓練を受ける機会を得ることが有給の仕事への就業に与える影響を調べる。

The revision connects the ideas in natural prose. Its form is one option for this passage.
