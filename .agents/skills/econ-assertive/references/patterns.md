# Content selection examples

These original examples use [default-micro](default-micro.md): Gershenson et al. (2022), Table 2, estimates the effect of Black students having at least one Black teacher in grades K-3 on college enrollment. The IV estimate is 5.9 percentage points, its standard error is 2.7 percentage points, and mean enrollment in the sample is 31.3%.

## Requested answer

Request: report the estimate and its uncertainty.

Answer: "Gershenson et al. (2022) estimate the effect of Black students having at least one Black teacher in grades K-3 on college enrollment. The IV estimate is 5.9 percentage points, with a standard error of 2.7 percentage points."

## Delete the full proposition

The following draft fragments add rejected meanings to that answer. Delete their entire content.

| Rejected draft fragment | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 「この結果はあらゆる教育政策の効果を保証しない。」 | Unasked objection |
| "This explanation focuses on the reported result within the study's setting." | Affirmative coverage declaration |
| 「これは単なる進学率の変化ではなく、教育機会を根本的に変える成果である。」 | Rhetorical contrast and promotion |
| "A cautious reader might say that the result offers only preliminary guidance." | Attributed caveat and evaluation |
| "If broader applications are questioned, these findings still provide valuable insights." | Hypothetical rebuttal and praise |

## Delete a clause inside an admitted sentence

Draft: "The IV estimate is 5.9 percentage points (standard error 2.7 percentage points), an encouraging result even though broader policy questions remain."

Answer: "The IV estimate is 5.9 percentage points, with a standard error of 2.7 percentage points."

The estimate and uncertainty answer the request. Delete the appended evaluation, concession and coverage claim. Apply the same deletion to a definition, question, heading, parenthesis or footnote carrying that meaning.
