# Selecting an explanation

This fictional example contains an oversubscribed apprenticeship program, a lottery assigning available places, subsequent employment records and detailed regression settings.

An account of the comparison:

> We compare later employment among lottery winners and losers to estimate how an offer of apprenticeship training changes applicants' access to paid work.

> 定員を超える応募者の中から受講者を抽選で選ぶ制度を利用し、当選者と落選者のその後の就業を比較する。これにより、職業訓練を受ける機会を得ることが有給の仕事への就業に与える影響を調べる。

The fixed classification and regression settings belong in the research specification. A short explanation needs the variation and comparison; repeating every available methodological fact obscures them. The target reader's question determines which facts matter.
