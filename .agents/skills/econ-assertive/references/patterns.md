# Economics wording examples

These are constructed editing examples, not empirical findings. Use only the example relevant to the current wording decision. Rewrite from the supplied evidence; do not import an example's numbers or mechanism.

## Direct findings

Input evidence: an estimated effect of 1.2 percentage points.

Japanese draft: 「本分析の結果は、政策が参入を促進する可能性を示唆しているといえる。」
Rewrite: 「参入率への推定効果は1.2パーセントポイントである。」

English draft: "These findings offer valuable insights into the potentially important role of the policy in fostering entry."
Rewrite: "The estimated effect on entry is 1.2 percentage points."

Delete praise and layers of distancing. Retain any supplied interval with the estimate when it belongs in the requested result. Do not append a generic warning about interpretation.

## Empty significance

Evidence: the model links entry costs to firm entry.

Japanese draft: 「本研究は、複雑な市場のダイナミクスに新たな光を当てる。」
Rewrite: 「本研究は参入費用が企業の参入に与える影響を分析する。」

English draft: "This study sheds new light on the intricate dynamics of market entry."
Rewrite: "The model relates entry costs to firm entry."

The claim identifies an economic object. A claim of novelty requires the literature evidence supplied by the research task.

## Defensive additions

Request: define an outcome recorded as police-recorded offenses.
Draft: "The outcome is recorded offenses. However, recorded offenses are not all actual crimes."
Rewrite: "The outcome is the number of police-recorded offenses."
Japanese: 「アウトカムは犯罪認知件数である。」

Delete the second proposition. Do not move it to a footnote, a measurement-limits paragraph or a supposedly positive scope statement. If the definition is already established and the question is about a coefficient, omit the repeated definition too.

Request: audit identification. Evidence: the treatment changes the reporting threshold from A to B.
Rewrite: "The treatment changes the reporting threshold from A to B."
Japanese: 「処置によって報告基準がAからBに変わる。」

This sentence answers the audit question directly. It needs no cautionary preface.

## Rhetorical opposition

Draft: "The contribution is not merely a new dataset; it is a fundamentally new lens on firm behavior."
Evidence: records identify firm entry and exit separately.
Rewrite: "The records distinguish firm entry from firm exit."
Japanese: 「この記録では企業の参入と退出を区別できる。」

Delete the staged opposition and the promotional claim. Keep substantive treatment/control comparisons and differences between economic hypotheses.

## Stable terms and literal mechanisms

Keep one name for a defined object. Do not alternate labor supply, work effort and employment when the source measures hours worked. Keep percentage changes and percentage-point changes distinct.

Japanese draft: 「固定効果が交絡を吸収し、識別の解像度を高める。」
Evidence: the fixed effects remove time-invariant municipality differences.
Rewrite: 「自治体固定効果は自治体間の時間不変の差を除く。」

English draft: "By leveraging rich fixed effects, the analysis robustly captures the nuanced causal landscape."
Rewrite: "Municipality fixed effects remove time-invariant differences across municipalities."

A concrete actor is not necessarily a person. 「価格の上昇は需要を減らす」 is ordinary economics prose when supported by the model or evidence. Keep "robust standard errors", "financial leverage" and mathematical symbols in their technical senses.

## Paragraphs and literature

Replace "important background -> three generic points -> this distinction matters" with the retained economic claim and its evidence. Join fragments when they express one argument. Split a sentence when it contains distinct claims. Keep genuine lists and requested structures. Do not engineer irregular sentence lengths or synonym substitutions to imitate a person.

A literature paragraph states the paper's relationship to the closest work through a specific question, mechanism, comparison, measurement or parameter. Attach each citation to the claim it supports. Delete chronological name lists and stock "fills a gap" conclusions. An introduction introduces the paper's question, approach and findings early; its paragraphs serve the argument. A paper explanation retains its five-section format.

## Editing boundary

"The model predicts lower entry." "The authors attribute the change to search costs." "The regression estimates a conditional association." "The theorem requires rank Q = k."

Each sentence states its actual research object directly. Changing the object changes the substance. Keep negative findings and conditions that the argument needs. Do not add a separate limits discussion or soften neighboring claims.

## Sources and adaptation

Reviewed 2026-10-01. These examples and the local rules are an original economics-specific synthesis. No upstream code or installer is used.

- [nanaism/yomiyasu](https://github.com/nanaism/yomiyasu/blob/main/SKILL.md): subject-predicate repair, removal of empty framing and figurative padding. Local rules omit fixed sentence-length and bullet quotas, automatic linting and improvement reports.
- [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer/blob/main/SKILL.md), version 3.1.0 as read: removal of staged contrasts, repeated closers, forced triads and inflated language. Local rules use economics register and return only the requested object.
- [softaworks/writing-clearly-and-concisely](https://github.com/softaworks/agent-toolkit/blob/main/skills/writing-clearly-and-concisely/SKILL.md): concrete subjects, active verbs, paragraph unity and deletion of needless words. Local rules omit automatic subagent editing.
- [Keizai Seminar introduction article](https://note.com/keisemi/n/n6442eac8af25): public article, six-part diagram and displayed before/after literature paragraphs. Use functional paragraphs and a specific connection to the closest literature. The password-protected supplements were not used.
- [John H. Cochrane, Writing Tips for Ph.D. Students](https://www.fma.org/assets/docs/membercontent/writing_cochrane.pdf): introduction guidance on leading with the contribution and the fact behind it. Use the economic content in place of generic importance statements.

The zero-default rule for hedges, concessions, qualifications and emphasis is the user's instruction. It governs the adaptations above. References supply editing ideas, not a competing instruction layer or proof of detector performance.
