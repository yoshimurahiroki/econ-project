# Revision examples

Use an example for the relation it clarifies. The shared operating rules belong to [econ-assertive](../SKILL.md).

## Information selection and numerical precision

Fictional fixture from [default-micro](default-micro.md). Request: report the estimate and its uncertainty.

Draft:

> In the fictional study, joint activation reduces peak demand by an additional 1.3 megawatts relative to private activation, with a 95 percent confidence interval of 0.85 to 1.75 megawatts, an encouraging result even though broader policy questions remain.

Revision:

> In the fictional study, joint activation reduces peak demand by an additional 1.3 megawatts relative to private activation. The 95 percent confidence interval is 0.85 to 1.75 megawatts.

The population, treatment, outcome, estimate, estimator and uncertainty remain. The evaluation and policy caveat are deleted.

## Connected explanation

Fictional fixture: an oversubscribed apprenticeship program. Request: explain the comparison. No causal estimate has been produced.

Draft:

> Applicants enter a lottery. Some receive a training place. We observe later employment. We compare the groups.

Revision:

> We compare later employment among lottery winners and losers to estimate how an offer of apprenticeship training changes applicants' access to paid work.

The sequence connects the comparison and its source of variation. The paragraph describes the comparison without adding an estimated effect.

## Concept density and necessary explanation

Constructed fixture: an annual workshop-output panel. Request: explain the descriptive quantity.

Draft:

> The baseline-group fixed-workshop publication-adjusted per-workshop output is the descriptive quantity.

Revision:

> For each group, we divide reported output by its fixed baseline workshop count. The fixed denominator lets the reader compare reported quantities without a changing workshop count in the denominator. We report publication coverage alongside the quantity because an unpublished cell does not establish zero output.

The definition, fixed denominator and publication state are distinct claims in one explanatory paragraph. The final sentence states the measurement relation needed to read the quantity.
