# Information and expression

Use these rules while authoring. The policy caller takes the completed method draft as input to one final whole-text pass. Identify each independent assertion, its supporting evidence and its defining conditions. Order retained assertions by what the reader must already know to understand the next one. Reconstruct the retained claims into the requested text under the rules below, including headings, notes, tables and examples. Read the reconstructed text in order before delivery. Repair independent-claim mergers and restore defining scientific conditions from the established content. These rules govern all generated prose; profile loading adds genre-specific source relations.

Retain the requested answer, result or action; explicit user requirements; and the definitions, evidence, reasoning, mechanisms, quantities, comparisons, conditions, assumptions, causal status and statistical uncertainty that determine its meaning. Select information by its contribution to the requested question or decision. Retain a source fact when omitting it would obscure that claim, its evidence or its defining condition. Material needed only to execute another research stage stays in that stage's records. A detail can leave an overview while remaining in a requested appendix or linked canonical record that supplies its evidence or reproducibility. Preserve the premise and reasoning needed to understand the main claim. Develop the explanation needed to understand each important fact. Concision means necessary information with low reading effort.

Delete all other content. Remove hedging, concessions, rhetorical qualification, ornament, routine caution, defensive disclaimers, generic warnings and limitations, unsupported uncertainty, promotion, self-assessment, process narration, empty transitions and repetition by meaning. Do not restore deleted meaning through affirmative rewording, attribution or hypothetical objections.

State claims at the logical status and strength established by their evidence. Keep a proposition's defining scientific conditions beside it, including in summaries of any length; use the same claim strength as the detailed argument. Express numerical uncertainty through the available estimate, standard error, interval, test or probability. State an exact unresolved value when it defines the requested object or changes a stated claim's interpretation.

Lead with the central answer, question or result in terms the reader can understand. Order the remaining information by what must be known to understand the next claim. Introduce a necessary concept before using it in an inference. Give each section a needed role, merge repeated explanations and place supporting detail where it explains the claim. Use headings, lists and tables when their organization helps the reader.

Give each sentence one substantive claim. Keep its defining condition and causal relation with that claim. Coordinate quantities that express one comparison; split clauses making independent definitions, findings, inferences or operations. Make the subject and predicate clear, replace dense noun chains with explicit relations, and give necessary terms an intelligible first use. Keep names for the same economic object stable and make pronoun referents clear. Vary sentence length and syntax with the information; remove mechanical openings and structures without fragmenting the argument.

Give each paragraph one specific claim and state it first. A shared actor or a broad label such as data, model, institution, mechanism, design or literature does not unite independent assertions. A sentence answering a different question belongs in another paragraph when the task needs it, and is otherwise deleted. Develop it through the evidence, explanation, mechanism, quantity or interpretation it needs. Each following sentence adds a substantive relation to the preceding sentence or central claim. Carry the established object into the next new fact; show causation, comparison, support or specification in the content. Choose paragraph length from its explanatory work. End when that claim is explained. Remove repetitions of the opening question or paragraph purpose, including closing sentences that simply return to what the study examines.

## Writing profiles

Profile loading is triggered by selected econ-writing or econ-edit work, the bridge's corresponding writing or wording-edit method, or an explicit task profile. Saving a research answer as text does not change its method or trigger a writing profile.

For that triggered work, select the explicitly named task profile. Otherwise read the export-project:v1 block of [project context](../../../docs/ai/repo_context.md) and select its writing_profile value; use [default-micro](references/default-micro.md) when that persistent selection is absent.

Read the profile functions required by the requested text and reuse loaded material. Default-micro supplies a required function absent from the selected profile. Transfer argument and information placement to target evidence; source-study facts stay in their examples. Preserve the requested language, format, citation system and notation.

[econ-style](../econ-style/SKILL.md) owns requested profile creation or revision. Creating or revising a profile preserves the persistent selection. Change writing_profile only for an explicit request to adopt or switch that selection.

Read [the revision examples](references/patterns.md) when an example is needed to resolve mixed factual and rhetorical content, fragmented explanation or concept density. [Source records](../../../docs/ai/sources.md) retain inspected versions and adaptations.
