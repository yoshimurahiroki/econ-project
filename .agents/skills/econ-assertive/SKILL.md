# Economics prose

## Purpose and input

For a new economics task, complete the brief [workflow entry](../econ-workflow/SKILL.md#task-entry) once, then continue here. Support calls within an existing task do not restart entry.

Write clear, natural economics prose for the requested reader, purpose and format.

Input: Requested text, reader, purpose, medium, research sources and selected writing profile.

## Common prose rule

State economic facts, objects, definitions, comparisons, equations and quantities directly and accurately. Preserve measured uncertainty and distinguish an observed association, a hypothesis and an estimated effect through the claim's subject, verb, comparison and magnitude. Define populations, mathematical domains and identifying conditions in the economic object or equation to which they belong.

Omit propositions that serve as reservations, concessions, hedging, caveats, scope qualifications, caution, conservatism, ornament or supplementary commentary or annotations. Apply this prohibition to their meaning across prose, slides, captions, notes and narration. Do not paraphrase, relocate or restore an excluded proposition by calling it necessary, useful, scientific or reader-specific. The sole automatic exception to this rhetorical-proposition prohibition is the wording of a verbatim source title or exact quotation already required by the task; do not introduce a quotation to invoke this exception. Propose every other exception to this rhetorical-proposition prohibition to the user with its exact sentence and reason, and obtain approval before using it.

Never include research-project plans, progress, stages, decision or adoption status, or future user decisions in research artifacts. This ban covers every form, including explanations, annotations and paraphrases. Do not apply the title or quotation exception to this categorical ban.

When editing instructions, retain prohibition and regulation instructions as operative rules; do not turn them into research-artifact commentary. Keep operational safeguards, source checks, approval requests and task coordination outside research artifacts; carry out the safeguards that govern the work.

## Procedure

### Select the economic argument

Choose content by the economic question and contribution the reader needs to understand. Prioritize the central question, mechanism, decisive comparison, main findings and contribution; connect institutions, data and methods through their roles in that argument. In general explanations and talks, give low default priority to analyses that revisit the same question with a modified specification, sample or assumption to establish that the main finding persists; recognize this robustness role by meaning, regardless of its label. Appendix placement is a weak signal of secondary importance; the finding's role in the argument governs selection. Select supporting findings for what they establish in the whole argument, rather than because they are correct or available: evidence that distinguishes competing mechanisms, answers a different economic question or establishes the central comparison advances the explanation. Answer an explicitly requested robustness analysis through the relevant research method. Omit repetition, technical inventories and internal management information. Do not invent facts, results, numbers or citations.

Let the reader, purpose and medium determine the explanation. Assess content, explanatory density, argument, sentences and vocabulary in complete connected passages. An Abstract concentrates on the main answer; an Introduction develops the reasoning that makes the answer intelligible. Use the function that serves the requested artifact. Stop when the passage conveys its economic content.

### Establish facts and choose the prose model

Read the paper, appendix, data, code and institutional sources needed to establish the facts. Use technical sections as evidence for general exposition. When writing a paper's specialist section, use econ-writing to select a corresponding-section prose model; do not transfer that section's conventions to general exposition. Express technical results through defined objects, equations and quantities. Choose Japanese terms and sentence connections in the context of the economic object, audience and medium.

### Select the profile

For worked prose examples, use the explicitly requested writing profile. Otherwise use the writing_profile in [project context](../../../docs/ai/repo_context.md), or [default-micro](references/default-micro.md) when no preference is recorded. Read the relevant complete passages and choose the target text's content and form for its own purpose. Use [revision examples](references/patterns.md) to examine information selection and expression.

### If profile creation or switching is requested

Use [econ-style](../econ-style/SKILL.md) for requested profile creation or revision. Saving or revising a profile preserves the persistent selection. Change writing_profile only when the user asks to adopt or switch it.

## Output

Economics prose that conveys the selected economic content for the requested purpose.
