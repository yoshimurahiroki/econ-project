# Economics prose

Select content before drafting by the economic question and contribution the reader needs to understand. Give central insights sufficient explanation and omit technically correct supplements that do not advance the argument. Retain a condition or comparison when the central economic insight depends on it. Let the reader, purpose and medium determine the explanation. Judge content, explanatory density, argument, sentences and vocabulary through complete connected passages that make the economic question, comparison, findings and contribution intelligible. Abstracts model compression around the main answer; Introductions model the reasoning needed to understand it. Select either function for the requested artifact rather than imposing Abstract density everywhere. Connect claims and evidence naturally. Choose the supported level of a claim before wording it: an observed association, a hypothesized mechanism or an estimated effect. Express that level through the subject, verb, comparison and magnitude. A correctly stated association does not need a second sentence denying causality; a descriptive result does not need a list of outcomes it cannot establish. Choose the economic comparison or benchmark the discovery changes, beyond identifying its setting or policy, before choosing supporting analyses. Once the comparison and finding are intelligible, omit routine technical justifications and secondary findings rather than completing the source’s coverage. Remove unnecessary propositions rather than rewriting them as qualifications, moving them to notes or appending what the result does not establish. Remove repetition, ornament, ritual hedging and concessions, defensive caveats and internal management information. Stop when the needed content is conveyed. Do not invent facts, results, numbers or citations.

Read the full paper, appendix, data, code or institutions as needed to establish facts. Their technical sections supply evidence, not models for general exposition. Choose natural Japanese terminology and connections for the economic object, audience and medium. Use contextual Japanese examples to resolve expression; the target argument determines prose density and presentation structure. A requested technical analysis retains the detail it needs. When writing a paper's specialist section, econ-writing additionally selects a corresponding-section prose model; that model does not govern general exposition.

For writing or revision that needs worked examples, use an explicitly requested writing profile; otherwise use the writing_profile in [project context](../../../docs/ai/repo_context.md), or [default-micro](references/default-micro.md) when no preference is recorded. Read relevant complete profile passages as examples, and choose the target text's content and form for its own purpose. [Revision examples](references/patterns.md) are available when useful.

[econ-style](../econ-style/SKILL.md) handles requested profile creation or revision. Saving or revising a profile preserves the persistent selection; change writing_profile only when the user asks to adopt or switch it.
