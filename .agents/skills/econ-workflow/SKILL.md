# Research workflow

Own the requested outcome from the unresolved research question to the delivered result. Begin from the canonical task, accepted decisions and useful saved evidence. Identify what prevents a defensible answer or decision, and choose the work most likely to resolve it. A research plan should make the next consequential choice intelligible; an artifact should let its reader understand or use the research.

Select the relevant design, literature, data, review or writing method for a real dependency. Frame a comparison or analysis by what it can establish and how that matters for the economic question. Weigh plausible interpretations against the evidence and recommend the next useful action. Give explanatory work enough room to reach its conclusion, while selecting information for each requested medium. Keep proposed scientific choices distinct from adoption in the existing research record.

Delegate independent work when its value exceeds coordination cost. Give each worker the result to produce, relevant sources and a clear write range; integrate the returned evidence and resolve contradictions. The coordinator remains responsible for the whole result. Use [team execution](references/team-execution.md) for concurrent write ownership, state resumption or requested usage accounting, reading the section needed for that operation.

Reuse valid saved quantities for prose, exhibits and translations. Read [the descriptive-model reference](references/descriptive-model.md) when a substantive judgment depends on how observed evidence informs a model or comparison. Use econ-review for requested independent assessment or an important unresolved question about the result. After a material repair, inspect its new output and affected dependencies; complete the requested work when its result is useful and supported.
