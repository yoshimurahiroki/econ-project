# Research workflow

Use this skill to coordinate a broad question or authorized project advance. Ordinary bounded tasks go directly to their specialist. Coordination and the primary method for each deliverable coexist. Enter at the requested stage. Read the relevant canonical task, accepted decisions, input versions and completed outputs. Reuse the existing evidence and results that establish the requested answer. Apply the method for the specified operation. Keep the researcher's unresolved choices in the existing research record.

For a requested research-wide plan, organize work around the central economic question and the consequential decision still to be made. Connect the actor's choice or economic relationship to the comparison, the records and measures that make it observable, the evidence needed to establish the claim, and the conclusion those facts and assumptions support. Explain the link at each step. Rank work by what it resolves for that question, rather than by the available files or a desire to complete every stage.

Choose the next operation from the unresolved dependency that changes the central conclusion or research decision. For each proposed analysis or exhibit, state the comparison it establishes and the decision or interpretation it supplies. Distinguish an observed pattern, a causal result and a model-based implication through their actual supporting evidence and assumptions. Carry the same definitions, quantities, source versions and adoption state into the requested artifacts; use econ-writing for their media-specific realization. Keep an unfinished result at its documented stage.

For requested descriptive work, connect the question or decision to the necessary comparison, saved input, observation unit and denominator. Compute the needed quantities once. Generate requested text, tables, figures and language variants from those quantities. Change calculation when inputs or definitions change; change presentation when wording or layout changes. Read [the Mahoney framework](references/descriptive-model.md) when selecting evidence for requested descriptive or model-based work.

For a requested plan or resumption, use the following stages to identify the technical dependency needed for that plan. When the requested deliverable is a stage's substantive output, select that stage's primary method from the active method index.

- Question: develop or assess the specified economic object, mechanism, population and outcome. Use econ-design.
- Literature: find or assess the specified work and contribution. Use econ-literature.
- Design and measurement: connect the requested comparison and assignment to documented fields and records needed for feasibility.
- Descriptive evidence: produce the requested comparison, support measure or distribution.
- Analysis decisions: record adopted samples, treatment, outcomes, transforms, estimator, weights and inference when the task changes those choices. Preserve the timing of confirmatory and exploratory choices.
- Implementation and interpretation: run the affected existing path or interpret its saved result. Use econ-data or econ-review.
- Writing: produce the requested argument or artifact. Use econ-writing.
- Transfer: carry the current task through econ-handoff when a handoff is requested.

For requested research planning, choose data-first or model-first by the uncertainty the next step resolves. Finish descriptive, causal-effect or model work at the requested result.

## Coordinated research

Frame the next consequential research decision before dividing work. Connect the economic mechanism, institutional variation, measurement and evidence to competing explanations. Prioritize work by its research consequence, information gained, dependencies unlocked and resource requirement. An issue tree organizes questions; assignment evidence establishes identification.

Cycle through problem framing, issue structuring, hypothesis design, prioritization, execution, synthesis, review, communication and update as the evidence changes. Execute authorized specialist work and integrate its actual outputs. For each unresolved scientific choice, give a recommendation, comparator, evidence and consequence in the canonical research record. Keep proposed changes separate from adopted choices. Continue independent implementation while a scientific adoption is pending.

At synthesis, match each selected comparison's quantitative deliverables and defining conditions to its source specification and specialist return. Preserve the needed components in the integrated assignment and completion evidence. Resolve missing components before advancing the comparison.

Read [team execution](references/team-execution.md) when delegating, maintaining multi-step state, selecting an execution tool or evaluating team performance. The coordinator owns dependency resolution, integration, resource allocation and final completion. Start only independent assignments within the verified runtime's available slots. Assign separate write ranges; a worktree separates changes and does not establish access isolation.

Use [econ-review](../econ-review/SKILL.md) for independent scientific and editorial assessment. Its [oversight entry points](../econ-review/references/oversight.md) distinguish proactive Inspector checks from independent Adjudicator decisions. Trigger inspection at consequential delegation, repeated failure, missing progress, artifact generation and handoff. Return a demonstrated defect to its responsible input, code, method, routing or evaluation and check a newly generated output under the same conditions.
