# Sources and literature

## Purpose and input

For a new economics task, complete the brief [workflow entry](../econ-workflow/SKILL.md#task-entry) once, then continue here. Support calls within an existing task do not restart entry.

Find literature, assess novelty, verify citations or institutions, and update a requested research source index.

Input: Specified materials, existing source records and requested search or verification scope.

Apply [the common prose rule](../econ-assertive/SKILL.md#common-prose-rule) to every output from this task.

## Procedure

### Search and assess the closest work

Start with the specified materials and existing records for those sources. Search the personal collection and academic discovery sources to cover the requested scope. Search by mechanisms and parameters as well as topics. Follow references, citing papers, authors, journals and repositories to locate work on the question. Use catalogs to locate papers and primary text to establish findings.

Use econ-paper to explain a paper's economic question and contribution. Establish a novelty judgment by reading the closest papers and comparing their questions, mechanisms, estimands, treatments, populations, variation, measurement and economic contributions. For an exhaustive search, retain queries, sources, versions and reviewed records in the existing research index and reconcile the specified collection. Keep search progress and project-management records outside research artifacts.

### Verify passages, versions and institutional facts

Verify a citation or finding against the supporting primary passage in the requested version. Resolve that version's DOI, citation key or stable ID. Establish publication facts from the publisher or official working-paper record. Establish institutional facts from official rules and operations. Compare exact passages to resolve source differences. Distinguish an announcement date, an effective date and an implementation date in claims about institutional timing.

Render citations in the artifact's requested format. Keep reading editions, claim locators and new-calculation inputs in existing evidence metadata. Preserve the bibliography and research PDFs. Store derived notes separately from holdings metadata. Use native connectors for live private records.

### If AI-method adoption is requested

For a requested AI-method adoption, read [task resources](../../../docs/ai/resources.md). Inspect the selected primary source, license, implementation files and dependencies. Keep adoption recommendations and status in operational records outside research artifacts.

### Output the literature’s economic relevance

Explain how the nearest literature bears on the research question, comparison or measurement. Identify the supporting passages and versions. Establish contribution from the papers' economic arguments and evidence; do not infer it from an author list or journal rank.
