# Oversight notes

## Scope and evidence

Assess the requested outcome from the available evidence. Execution checks establish file ownership, operation results and input/output agreement. Expert review judges whether that evidence supports the economic conclusion and intended use. Do not treat a completed state record or successful build as a substantive verdict. Record the exact review scope in the operational record.

## Review findings and independence

Use independent assessment when requested or needed for a consequential unresolved judgment or a specified comparison; it is not an automatic consequence of delegation. State the exact review question and whether it concerns execution, scientific validity, editorial usefulness or comparison. Use a different worker or session and bind its declared role to its actual runtime identity. Verify relevant context freshness and prior exposure when the independence claim depends on them; record unavailable facts as unknown rather than certifying independence from different names alone.

Give that reviewer the raw result, relevant source input and task, including applicable user content and output constraints, without a desired verdict. For a blinded comparison, withhold method condition and prior assessment history, not these requirements. Assess the support for retained claims and the usefulness of the economic explanation within those requirements; do not require propositions the user excluded. Restrict source exposure or stage reader impressions only when an explicit comparison protocol requires it, not for ordinary scientific review. Identify a worker's later review of its own result as a self-check. Establish each review finding through an observed defect, its consequence and supporting evidence. Separate the observed defect from hypotheses about its cause and assess the strongest contrary evidence. Return the assessment and supporting evidence with limitations and unresolved disagreements; preserve it separately from producer revisions. The coordinator retains whole-result integration and authorized disposition.

## Repair and reassessment

Repair a defect in the source, calculation, assignment or presentation that produces it. Assess the repaired result and affected dependencies. Reuse unchanged valid evidence. Reassess a repeatedly failing approach instead of adding supervisory steps. Independently confirm a consequential defect in an evaluation before changing its criteria.

## Assessment target and authority

Assess a scientific claim through its strongest evidence. Assess an editorial artifact through its argument and the intended reader's understanding. Leave scope, scientific choices and permissions with the user and their governing records. Keep review assignments, project plans, progress, execution stages, user decisions and adoption status in operational records outside research artifacts. Do not turn these assessment purposes into an automatic sequence of additional roles.
