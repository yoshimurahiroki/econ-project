# Economics writing profiles

## Purpose and input

For a new economics task, complete the brief [workflow entry](../econ-workflow/SKILL.md#task-entry) once, then continue here. Support calls within an existing task do not restart entry.

Create or revise an economics writing profile for the requested language, reader and medium.

Input: Requested reader, purpose, languages and products; supplied complete examples and existing profile.

Apply [the common prose rule](../econ-assertive/SKILL.md#common-prose-rule) to research artifacts produced by this task. Carry out source-verification, authorization and comparison safeguards outside those artifacts.

## Procedure

### Select and verify source passages

Define the profile's reader, purpose, languages and intended products. Read the supplied complete examples and existing profile, then obtain the additional material the request requires. Choose examples that connect the economic question, comparison, central findings and contribution. Assess each passage's content and expression against the explanation its reader needs. Select empirical and theoretical arguments to demonstrate distinct writing choices. Use final authoritative editions for source-derived examples, verify the edition and record the complete passage boundaries. Do not treat a fragment or an earlier version as evidence of a complete later passage.

Read each passage's connected argument, substantive subsections, exhibits and relevant footnotes. Inspect page images when extraction obscures the text. Establish facts, mechanisms, results and citations from the underlying research and appendices. Keep specialist-section conventions separate from general exposition. Choose Japanese terms and sentence connections in context. Let the requested product determine organization and explanatory density.

### Derive principles and complete examples

Distinguish a summary's concentration on the answer from an opening's developed reasoning. Identify the economic information each passage selects, the role of its setting and comparison, and the insight established by its evidence. Explain how the sentences connect that information. Identify omitted technical material by its role in the argument. Write high-level principles and complete original examples that demonstrate these choices. An Abstract and an Introduction can show different treatments of the same research content. Do not impose fixed paragraph templates, word bans, length limits or item inventories. Do not attribute example-specific facts to the user's study.

### If an opening is requested from a study body

For a requested opening based on a study body, save a reusable brief of the body and its section relationships. Retain central and supporting technical material in the brief; do not preselect the opening's content. Keep the original Abstract, Introduction and their analysis outside the author's input. Save the argument plan and complete prose before opening the same-edition reference. Compare content selection, economic reasoning and expression. Identify central content lost and peripheral or weakening propositions added. Use the fixed brief for an authorized revision and preserve separate inputs for independent confirmation.

### If a writing comparison is requested

For a requested writing comparison, preserve the exact task and allowed inputs. Save complete drafts before reading a reference answer. Assess the economic content, reasoning and expression of each draft, including alternative arguments. Attribute omissions to absent evidence, content selection or expression. Retain original outputs and decision records when revising the profile. Do not count feedback used in a revision as independent confirmation. Use the existing task-verification procedure when the requested work calls for a comparison; do not add comparisons to ordinary writing tasks.

### Save the profile and its private evidence

Keep input identities, versions, reading scope and detailed comparison records in the private research or evaluation area. Save the principles and original worked passages at the requested profile destination. Label fictional examples and invented quantities. Preserve required third-party notices. For an authorized profile test, use representative target prose with fixed facts and base the profile revision on the resulting writing.

### Preserve or explicitly switch the selected profile

A repository-save request without a path updates docs/ai/custom-style.md after reading it. [econ-assertive](../econ-assertive/SKILL.md) owns profile selection. Profile creation or revision preserves the writing_profile in [project context](../../../docs/ai/repo_context.md). Change it only when the user asks to adopt or switch the profile.

## Output

The requested principles and original worked passages at the specified profile destination.
