# Paper explanation

Read the specified version and the evidence supporting its main claims at the requested depth. Keep exact editions and passage locations in existing source metadata.

Select the paper's answer for the requested reader, keeping the requested question, significance, method, results and contribution. In a general empirical explanation, the method is the design's source of variation, identification method, comparison and outcome. The results are the principal findings and their economic meaning. Omit routine estimation settings, identification conditions and robustness checks from this account; use them for a requested methodological appraisal.

For example, Kim (2021)'s design can be explained as follows:

> 2010年後半から2013年後半に処方履歴の照会義務を導入した10州と、2016年末まで導入しなかった34州について、差の差法（DID）で導入前後の変化を比較する。2003–2016年のオピオイド供給量と薬物別の過量摂取死亡率を分析する。

This selects the comparison that answers the question from a source that also describes controls, coding and inference. Choose the equivalent explanation for the target paper; explain a distinctive design feature when it is central to that answer.
