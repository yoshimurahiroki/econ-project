# From research records to a paper

Use for a complete paper, a paper-wide argument plan or a substantive whole-paper redraft. For a requested specialist section, use the section-composition guidance without creating an unnecessary whole-paper plan. Econ-writing owns the integrated manuscript. [Econ-assertive](../../econ-assertive/SKILL.md) owns content selection and direct prose; the selected profile supplies original examples. Use specialist methods for an actual unresolved research question, not as a compulsory writing sequence.

## Establish the argument from the research

Read the adopted research record and the main exhibits before choosing sections. Identify the economic decision and what the evidence changes in our understanding of it. Relate the actors' incentives, the institutional variation, the comparison and measurement to that contribution. Trace the central claims to their supporting records, including the magnitude and economic meaning of the main results. Select the headline result for the economic question it answers, not for how much of its calculation the input reproduces. A result explicitly reported by the source or saved research record can enter at that documented level; recreating its calculation is a separate task. Keep a missing verification step in the work record rather than replacing the main answer with a secondary statistic. This working account supplies the manuscript; it is not a checklist to print in it.

A pre-estimation study can establish a question, mechanism, institutional comparison, measurement and descriptive facts. Write those parts from confirmed records. Keep unavailable results and unresolved scientific choices in the existing work record, outside manuscript prose. Do not fill a Results section with expected estimates or write a completed causal finding from descriptive patterns.

## Design a paper-wide argument

Choose the sections and their order from what the reader needs to understand and believe. For each section, specify its contribution to the central argument and the evidence that establishes it. A comparison may require an institutional explanation first; a new measurement may need to precede the design. These dependencies, rather than the order in which analyses were run, determine the paper.

The Introduction explains the entire economic argument. The body develops its evidentiary and methodological foundations. Give each important explanation a home: summarize its role in the Introduction, then develop it where the reader needs its details. Do not paste several independently written summaries together or repeat a full explanation in both places. When moving from a paper-wide plan to its Introduction, reselect the opening argument rather than summarize each planned section in turn. A sample-construction rule, specification detail or diagnostic can have a necessary home in the body without earning a sentence in the opening. A literature comparison belongs where it makes the added knowledge intelligible, rather than requiring a separate catalogue of papers.

## Compose the relevant section

Abstracts compress the question, decisive comparison, principal answer and economic meaning. Introductions explain why that question arises, how the comparison answers it and why the findings change existing knowledge. Write a paper's Abstract after its argument and findings are settled. Abstract compression does not set the depth of an Introduction, research plan or explanation.

Use the selected Abstract–Introduction examples for general exposition. Only while actually drafting a paper's specialist section, also read a relevant source's corresponding section as a prose model. Record its edition and section in the working source record. For example:

- An institutional section explains the rules that change actors' decisions or generate the comparison.
- A data section connects the economic object to the observed records, units and construction needed to measure it.
- An identification section develops the source of treatment, comparison, estimand and the design-specific conditions and implementation needed to establish that effect.
- A results section starts with the answer and its magnitude, then uses additional evidence to explain the answer's mechanism or meaning.

These are section functions, not a mandatory table of contents. Corresponding-section models remain local to that writing task; their technical density does not enter the general profile or a later paper explanation. Reading a full article to verify a fact never changes its role into a general prose exemplar. When the next requested product is a general explanation, return to its economic question and selected opening model; do not shorten the specialist section into that explanation.

## Turn the argument into connected prose

Give a paragraph a claim to establish. Develop its meaning, mechanism or evidence in the order the reader needs. Carry an identifiable actor, choice, comparison or finding from one sentence to the next; introduce a new object when the reasoning needs it. For example, a rule changes the supplier's expected cost; that cost changes a supply choice; the empirical comparison measures the affected margin. The relation must be supported by this study, not manufactured to make a smooth transition.

Use consistent terms for the same object, precise quantity comparisons and natural verbs. Sentence form follows meaning: passive voice can keep an important outcome in focus, and a direct statement of what something is can define a new object. A synonym change or connective cannot repair a missing logical relation.

## Revise the integrated manuscript

Read the assembled paper as one argument before polishing individual phrases. Resolve a missing reason from the available evidence, delete a digression, move an explanation to where it is needed, or rewrite a paragraph's role. Then check that claims and exhibits still support one another and that terms and referents carry through the manuscript. Use econ-edit for scoped textual revision and econ-review for an independent assessment when requested. A successful local paragraph edit is not evidence that the paper as a whole now works.
