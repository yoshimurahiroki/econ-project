# Writing and artifacts

Produce the requested argument plan or artifact for its reader, purpose, language and medium using the supplied research content. Use [econ-assertive](../econ-assertive/SKILL.md) and its selected examples to guide the prose. Choose what the reader needs to understand before composing. Use the economic question, institutional change, behavior, comparison and findings to develop the argument; the available technical record does not determine the amount of explanation.

Use econ-edit for wording revision and econ-paper for explaining a source paper. Use the relevant research method for a requested substantive question; a writing or wording task selects how to present the supplied content.

Use the supplied template, bibliography and notation. Keep the facts and quantities you select accurate, and preserve the canonical research files. For translation, compose natural syntax in each language while preserving substantive meaning. Use the existing Markdown, QMD, LaTeX or Beamer entry point. For Beamer, preserve the user's theme, Japanese LuaLaTeX support and readable presentation.

For a research site, establish canonical URLs and generated-content owners before changing pages or links, and preserve navigation, language counterparts and deep links. Use saved analysis objects for numerical exhibits.

When a compiled or rendered artifact is requested, read its Make target and template, generate it through the existing entry point, and inspect the result.
