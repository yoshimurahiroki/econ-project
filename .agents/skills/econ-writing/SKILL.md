# Writing and artifacts

Own the argument and finished artifact for the requested reader, purpose, language and medium. Use [econ-assertive](../econ-assertive/SKILL.md) and the selected profile for content selection and prose. For a complete paper, paper-wide argument plan or substantive whole-paper redraft, use [the paper workflow](references/paper-workflow.md): establish the central insight from research records and exhibits, design the section relationships, compose the required sections and revise the integrated manuscript. Coordinate those writing stages here; econ-workflow is needed when the request also advances interdependent research work.

For a bounded passage, talk, research plan or memo, develop the supplied economic argument at the depth its reader needs. Use Abstracts for concise selection and Introductions for developed reasoning. A paper's specialist section may additionally use a corresponding-section model under the paper workflow; its detail does not become the model for general exposition. Use econ-paper to explain a source and econ-edit for scoped revision. Unresolved scientific decisions belong to the relevant research method and the user's authorized scope.

Use the supplied template, bibliography and notation. A talk develops its own spoken argument for the audience and time available. Select each slide for what listeners need to understand at that point; the paper’s supporting evidence need not all appear in the opening. Keep the facts and quantities you select accurate, and preserve the canonical research files. For translation, compose natural syntax in each language while preserving substantive meaning. Use the existing Markdown, QMD, LaTeX or Beamer entry point. For Beamer, preserve the user's theme, Japanese LuaLaTeX support and readable presentation.

For a research site, establish canonical URLs and generated-content owners before changing pages or links, and preserve navigation, language counterparts and deep links. Use saved analysis objects for numerical exhibits.

When a compiled or rendered artifact is requested, read its Make target and template, generate it through the existing entry point, and inspect the result.
