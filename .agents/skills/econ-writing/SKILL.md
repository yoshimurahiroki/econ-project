# Writing and artifacts

## Purpose and input

For a new economics task, complete the brief [workflow entry](../econ-workflow/SKILL.md#task-entry) once, then continue here. Support calls within an existing task do not restart entry.

Design and write a complete economics paper from research records, or compose, translate and format a passage, plan, talk, site or exhibits.

Input: Requested reader, purpose, language and medium; research records, exhibits, selected profile and supplied template.

Apply [the common prose rule](../econ-assertive/SKILL.md#common-prose-rule) to every output from this task.

## Procedure

### Choose whole-paper or bounded composition

Compose the argument and artifact for the requested reader, purpose, language and medium. Use [econ-assertive](../econ-assertive/SKILL.md) and the selected profile to select content and write prose. For a complete paper, paper-wide argument plan or substantive whole-paper redraft, use [the paper workflow](references/paper-workflow.md). Establish the central insight from research records and exhibits, connect the sections to that insight, write the sections and revise the integrated manuscript. Coordinate the writing here. Use econ-workflow's coordination procedure for interdependent research tasks within this same task.

For a passage, talk or memo, develop the economic argument through its reasoning and evidence. Apply [econ-assertive's content-selection and density principles](../econ-assertive/SKILL.md#select-the-economic-argument). Use a corresponding-section model for a paper's specialist section under the paper workflow. Do not use specialist-section detail as the model for general exposition. Use econ-paper to explain a source and econ-edit for scoped revision. Resolve scientific questions through the relevant research method within the user's authorized scope.

### Keep operational plans separate

Keep project plans, progress, stages, decision or adoption status and future user decisions outside research deliverables. Do not insert them through explanations, annotations or paraphrases. Keep writing plans and unresolved scientific choices in the existing work record. Deliver an explicitly requested project work plan as an operational document separate from research text. Report a request that requires operational content inside a research deliverable as a conflict before drafting.

### Compose for the medium and language

Use the supplied template, bibliography and notation. Develop a talk's spoken argument for its audience and duration. Explain a mechanism by interpreting its reported behavioral comparisons together: identify the actor, action and margin. Select exhibits and spoken explanation from that account in the order listeners need. Select the opening evidence through the economic argument. Preserve the facts, quantities and canonical research files. Translate substantive meaning into natural syntax in each language. Use the existing Markdown, QMD, LaTeX or Beamer entry point. For Beamer, preserve the user's theme, Japanese LuaLaTeX support and readable presentation.

### If the artifact is a research site

For a research site, establish canonical URLs and generated-content owners before changing pages or links. Preserve navigation, language counterparts and deep links. Generate numerical exhibits from saved analysis objects.

### If a compiled or rendered artifact is requested

For a requested compiled or rendered artifact, read its Make target and template, generate it through the existing entry point and inspect the result.

## Output

The requested composed, translated or formatted artifact, with the research meaning and quantities preserved.
