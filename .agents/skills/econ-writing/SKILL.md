## Compose

Select the question, answer and decisive evidence from the established study or source materials. Give prominence to results that change the mechanism, action ranking, allocation or economic magnitude. Retain a non-effect, subgroup contrast or behavioral response when omitting it changes that interpretation. Build the explanatory relation before drafting a compressed Abstract, slide or summary. For a whole paper, consult [paper guidance](references/paper-workflow.md) for the required section architecture; load a specialist section only when the request uses it. Complete the common process in the resulting artifact.

## Revise

Start from the author's original text and the requested scope. Recover its intended propositions and source-supported relations, then repair information selection, logical order and paragraph connections before surface wording. Reconstruct unclear Japanese as a complete spoken explanation and shorten only after the reader can follow it. Preserve suitable original phrasing and apply the common review to the revised text with the context needed to assess its connections.

## Translate

Recover the source's claim, actor, mechanism and evidence before choosing target-language sentences. Use natural Japanese or English grammar, established technical terms and the requested register. Rebuild sentence boundaries and explanatory order where the source language's structure impedes comprehension. Keep quantities, assumptions, comparisons, citations and referenced findings; explain an implicit relation only when the source establishes it. Complete the common review against the source and final translation.

## Convert

Identify the settled argument and the information each unit of the target medium must communicate. Form the complete explanation first, then select what belongs in the paper, slide, speaker notes or research page. Preserve the claim, determining comparisons, units and canonical exhibits. Use the common process on the converted artifact and inspect the requested render when its layout is part of delivery.

## Guidance

Identify the concrete obstacle to understanding and explain the writing decision that resolves it. Use a source-bound revision of the supplied passage when available. Demonstrate how content selection and logical reconstruction produce the improved wording, then complete the common review of the advice and example. Return only the guidance needed for the requested decision.

## Explain

Explain the specified paper, theory, institution, method or result at the requested depth. Select the relevant question and answer, then develop the source-supported mechanism and evidence through the common process.

### Read and select the argument

Read the cited passage and the supporting material needed to understand its objects, premises, operations and result. Choose the information the reader needs to recover the answer. Use the requested audience or the adopted profile to decide which concepts need explanation.

### Develop the connected explanation

Connect actors, rules, choices, causal or theoretical mechanisms, identifying comparisons and numerical magnitudes in their logical order. For Japanese, form the complete explanation a reader can follow aloud, separate distinct topics, and then compress the established argument. Match the explanation to the source's actual result.

### Complete the requested explanation

Complete the integrated review of the final explanation, including its source correspondence and causal links. Return the answer and its decisive evidence at the requested depth.

## Profile

Create or update a reusable profile only when requested. Apply the common process to its principles and worked examples; the profile describes reusable writing decisions drawn from the selected sources.

### Select source passages

Choose examples that demonstrate distinct decisions on information selection, explanation order, sentence connection and wording. Read the passages themselves with the context required to understand those decisions.

### Derive principles and worked examples

Extract a practical rule from each necessary example and show the choice it resolves. Distinguish information selection observed in the published text from the reusable rule inferred from it; published order does not establish the author's internal sequence of thought. Preserve the source's meaning in any revision. Teach the operation that makes a claim understandable; fixed paragraph counts, phrase inventories and measured rhythms from a few samples supply no general style rule.

### Use a requested opening or comparison

Revise or continue the authorized content through the common process. Build the explanation from that content's own facts, then apply the relevant profile choice.

### Save the profile

Save the completed profile and necessary worked examples to the requested existing destination. Review the actual saved content as one deliverable and create a new file only when the requested reusable material needs it.

### Organize selective reading

Keep the profile's principles and example anchors usable for selective reading. Load the language and argumentative function that resolve the current writing choice.

## Compose for the medium and language

Use this process for every mode, scaled to the requested passage and artifact. These are substantive writing operations within one task, with no required intermediate transcript or separate review document.

1. Read the actual request, adopted research choices, source passage and applicable PROSE rules. Identify the reader, medium, editing scope and the answer the reader needs.
2. Select the claims, facts, comparisons and premises that establish that answer. Resolve their logical dependencies and the economic mechanism before drafting for a length target. Use [Select the economic argument](../../runtime/PROSE.md#select-the-economic-argument) and [Connect the explanation](../../runtime/PROSE.md#connect-the-explanation).
3. Build a complete connected explanation. In Japanese, first express it as a natural spoken explanation with the relevant actor, action, reason, mechanism and result. Separate distinct topics. Restore a missing link from the source or completed analysis. Keep this reconstruction within the requested source and editing scope.
4. Reshape the explanation for the requested academic language and medium using [Revise for the requested language](../../runtime/PROSE.md#revise-for-the-requested-language). Only now remove repetition, empty framing and other dispensable wording. Preserve the relations and quantities needed to understand the claim. A request for concision changes the final selection and phrasing, while the reasoning still reaches completion.
5. Put the completed wording in the requested text or artifact. Perform [the integrated editorial review](../../runtime/PROSE.md#finish-the-passage) once across all required aspects, repair the problems found and reread the affected passage. Finish the requested deliverable and use the existing verification path for any actual rendering or registration behavior changed by the task.

## Compose a talk

Give each slide one claim, its evidence and the information required for an audience to understand it. Derive the slide from the complete explanation; retain the premise or numerical comparison that makes its claim interpretable. Use speaker notes for the requested oral delivery. Match text and exhibit size to the actual slide.

## Compose a research site

Use the project's existing publication and analysis objects and preserve canonical links. Select the question, result and evidence each page needs, then adapt the complete explanation to the page's reading order.

## Render the requested artifact

Use the existing build path and view the actual resulting page or slide when layout affects delivery. Avoid unrelated rebuilds.

## Output

Return the completed text or artifact first, with the substantive explanation the request calls for. When the task is a rewrite, deliver the finished wording. Report actual changes and decisive evidence when a completion report is requested.

## Execute paragraph and argument contracts

Consult [paragraph guidance](references/paragraph-workflow.md) when a complex long-form argument needs structure. Perform its argument and paragraph reasoning in the draft and include it in the common integrated review. An explicitly selected formal certification or applicable platform contract activates its existing state, assignments and independent release review. Ordinary writing completes through the common process above.
