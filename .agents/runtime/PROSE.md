# Build the requested prose

Apply [CORE's primary expression rule](CORE.md) to every passage, including explanations, translations, captions and completion reports. This file owns its implementation in prose; the [dictionary](../quality/prose_rules.json) owns the existing lexical patterns and semantic criteria.

## Select the economic argument

Determine the question the reader needs answered, the answer, its supporting evidence and the economic relation connecting them. Read the actual source passages and adopted research choices needed to establish that relation. Carry forward facts, quantities, technical terms, citations and logical conditions. Complete this reasoning before shortening the text.

Give each paragraph one central proposition and the explanation that establishes it. Place premises before the inference that uses them, keep an explanation beside its claim, and give consequential steps the space they need. The argument determines paragraph length, heading structure and the number of examples. Delete thin paragraphs, generic introductions, repeated conclusions and a chronological account of the writer's efforts.

Write the substantive proposition directly. Remove reservations, concessions, disclaimers, preemptive rebuttals, declarations of conceptual distinctions, negative contrasts, empty importance claims and commentary on the writer's intentions or future work. Scientific precision, completeness and domain expertise supply no exemption. Rebuild the claim and paragraph; moving the same function into a footnote, parenthesis, quotation or closing paragraph preserves the defect.

## Connect the explanation

Name the actor, action, quantity and comparison that make the proposition concrete. In a mechanism, connect the institutional or informational change to incentives, choices and outcomes. In a theoretical argument, connect the defined objects and assumptions to the result through the decisive step. Use only relations established by the source or completed analysis; an attractive causal story adds no evidence.

Match each subject to a verb it can meaningfully perform. Name the actual researcher, firm, household or institution when agency matters; use a direct description of the state when no actor is needed. Replace personified documents and chains of abstract nouns with the operation or relation they describe. Restore an omitted intermediate step from the source when it carries the reasoning. Preserve established technical uses and keep one term for one concept.

Link sentences through their actual relation: cause, consequence, premise, example, sequence or measured comparison. Make referents explicit and place modifiers beside their targets. Each paragraph should advance the preceding argument and prepare the next necessary step. Remove a connector whose claimed relation the sentences fail to establish.

Existing ja-068, B28 and C04 cover expressions such as 「わけではありません」「意味しません」「区別が必要です」「実施されていません」 and their equivalents. Replace the whole construction with the definition, target population, measured comparison or actual operation that answers the question. A measured absence can be stated as its observed zero count; a missing measurement supplies no zero. Preserve the source's truth conditions and actual values. Judge the function of unlisted paraphrases during composition and the integrated review; a missing dictionary match never licenses the same rhetoric.

## Select the profile

Use an explicitly requested writing profile, otherwise the adopted project profile or [default-micro](../profiles/default-micro/PROFILE.md). Read an example only when it helps solve a concrete phrasing or explanatory choice. Avoid creating or changing a profile without a request.

## Revise for the requested language

For Japanese, reconstruct a complete explanation a reader can follow when spoken aloud: who acts, what changes, why, through which mechanism and with what result. Separate distinct topics and supply the necessary links from the source. Then remove repetition, empty framing and words that add no information. Retain the premises, mechanism, comparisons, units and technical terms needed to recover the claim. Oral reconstruction is a way to organize the explanation; the finished academic passage uses natural 常体 and the register of its paper, handout or talk.

Repair English-shaped word order, long subject–predicate gaps, ambiguous modification, excessive nominalization and unnatural inanimate agency. Translate the intended operation into natural Japanese instead of reproducing metaphors such as information being carried, a system opening something, or an argument living somewhere. In English, prefer precise ordinary verbs and direct constructions, including ordinary copulas, to inflated substitutes. Preserve the author's effective wording and established disciplinary terminology.

Read rhythm at paragraph scale. Join choppy sentences when they express one relation; divide a sentence where distinct claims or a long modifier obscure the point. Vary sentence length, order and endings as the argument requires. Remove mechanical triples, uniform paragraph molds, repeated openings or connectors, decorative emphasis, and redundant closing summaries. Use lists for actual parallel items or steps and tables for comparisons; write causal development in connected prose. Fixed length targets, banned-word totals and artificial variation do not establish natural writing.

Use the reader's knowledge to allocate explanation. The default economics profile assumes doctoral core-course knowledge and explains other fields' institutions and terms for an intelligent nonspecialist. Keep the requested audience and medium when specified. Create no new fact, finding, experience, opinion, citation or causal link to make the prose sound more human.

## Finish the passage

Always perform one substantive integrated editorial review of the actual completed text. Read its answer, source support, logical dependencies, explanation order, paragraph contributions, headings, sentence connections, prohibited rhetorical functions and natural rhythm together. Check that revision preserved the adopted claims, terms, numbers, units, comparisons, citations and editing scope. A word substitution or a zero-match scan leaves this review incomplete.

Repair every problem found and reread the affected passage with its connecting sentences until the requested text is complete. Consolidating review into one pass retains all these judgments. Deliver the finished wording or edit the requested artifact; extra drafts, review reports, ledgers and dictionary scans require a task that actually calls for them. Apply CORE's rule to additional verification.

## Hierarchical argument and paragraph composition

For a long paper or presentation, identify the central answer, arrange the necessary supporting claims and give each paragraph a function in that argument. Use [paragraph guidance](../skills/econ-writing/references/paragraph-workflow.md) to resolve actual structural choices. Carry out the reasoning and integration in the requested draft. Separate paragraph state, host assignments and independent release reviews belong to an explicitly selected formal workflow.
