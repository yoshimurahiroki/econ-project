# Select and solve the consequential problem

The lead owns formulation and adoption; specialists supply the needed evidence and calculations. Read the section resolving the pending decision.

## Turn the question into an operation

Identify the requested proposition, quantity, artifact or action, its consumer, fixed inputs and determining obstacle. Start the next authorized operation. New or exploratory research requires deepening before design; explanations use a discriminating comparison; substantive choices use feasible-action selection. Reuse an unchanged adopted formulation.

## Deepen a consequential research problem

Before adopting a new question, estimand or model, compare the initial question with its strongest consequential upstream formulation: whose objective, incentive, constraint, information or identifying variation determines the requested result? Make one additional substantive reconsideration, keeping the original deliverable and fixed specifications. Identify the premise that could invalidate dependent work and an observation, derivation or tractable operation distinguishing it. Retain the initial formulation when it resolves that margin.

Connect actors' objectives, feasible choices and information to behavior, equilibrium, welfare and adjustment. Derive the determining optimality, best-response or action-switching condition. Add substitution, entry, heterogeneity, dynamics or organizational responses only when they change the prediction. For diagnosis, connect the observed gap to its generating mechanism and an observable repair condition; compare the intervention's whole-objective effect, including displacement. Use the relevant [design method](../skills/econ-design/references/designs.md) and knowledge bodies for the derivation.

Bind the lead's applied comparison to the existing specification with `research_design.py formulate CONTRACT --root PROJECT --output OUTPUT`. Its `judgment.formulation` compares initial and alternative questions with their five economic dimensions and records the selected question and rationale. The saved definitions must match the specification. Assessment uses `candidates[].judgment.formulation`; reuse matching `adopted_design` records. Selection accepts `research_decisions.formulation={path,sha256}` and optional `candidate_id` for an assessment record. The producer validates these bindings.

Carry the selected formulation into its decisive operation. Reopen it only for a specific unresolved distinction changing the choice, rejected mechanism or binding constraint. Preserve authority and the task's resource limits.

## Separate explanations with a comparison

State consequential alternatives, their predictions under common inputs and an observation overturning the leading explanation. Choose the population, time, choice boundary or counterfactual where predictions differ. Set comparison groups and criteria before selecting a conclusion; retain an original comparison when an authorized premise changes. A numerical tolerance reflects the decision-relevant difference. Distinguish a bounded non-effect from an uninformative estimate; subgroup differences require their direct contrast.

Preserve the target, observation unit and decision-relevant distinctions through aggregation. Resolve contradictions at the determining source, measurement or model. When independent reasoning is requested, share definitions and inputs while keeping developing judgments separate until integration; agreement supplies no evidence. Stop when the consequential alternatives are separated or their relevant bounds established.

## Select a feasible action

For substantive economic or project choices, compare feasible candidates under the adopted objective, rights, resource constraints, population, period and units. Remove infeasible and dominated options, then select the highest-ranked feasible action. Do not combine unlike objectives into an unexplained score. Equivalent outcomes favor fewer additional resources. Use supported ordinal rankings or switching conditions when a numerical ranking is undetermined.

When information acquisition itself is a candidate action, compare the current best action with decisions after attainable signals under the same objective and supported probabilities. Include acquisition costs, delay and feasibility changes once. Deduct money from outcome amounts before utility, and utility-denominated costs from expected utility; transform costs consistently when changing currency or utility scale. Perfect information bounds partial-signal value only under the same costs and feasibility. Acquire information when its resulting objective exceeds the current action; otherwise act or retain the determining switching input. Ordinary working effort uses CORE's brief thought experiment.

Project choices compare complete feasible allocations, including displaced shared capacity, timing and execution costs. Information value already includes better downstream decisions; do not count their avoided losses again. Use existing `project-view --view forecast` for feasibility and `project_judgment` for the adopted comparison, sources, order and authority. On determining changes, use `project-impact` to update affected decisions and consumers. A proposed allocation does not amend fixed commitments.

## Source integration and method selection

Define the target and determining conditions before lookup. Derive an analytical implication when it directs the search or separates methods. Compare methods on the same target, data, information, economic restrictions and resource requirements; read the selected method's required knowledge before adopting it. Search further only for an unresolved claim, applicability condition, ranking or requested source coverage.

## Investigate source claims

Start with supplied sources and holdings. Select retrievals by the requested claims and competing predictions they can resolve. Match title, author, year, edition and stable identifier; resolve ambiguous identities. Follow determining backward references, forward citations, original observations and newly relevant terms. Copied or circular reports share their originating evidence.

Read the original passages, equations and tables establishing each claim and its population, period, quantities and conditions. Search snippets and unread citations are leads. An organization's statement establishes what it said; effects and quality require their underlying observations and comparison. Apply the same standard to every organization, including OpenAI. User insistence, prestige and a prior answer do not change evidence.

Retain exact source identity, version, location and inspected text through existing source/evidence owners; hash retained bytes when the task uses persistent bindings. Keep derived notes separate. Distinguish searched, found, inspected and inaccessible material. Update the unresolved claim and next useful lead in existing context; a failed search does not establish nonexistence.

Finish on the requested questions and coverage with determining conflicts resolved. Stop exhausted branches or an actual access/resource limit, retaining the exact unresolved passage and its effect on completion. Reopen a finished branch only for a new determining input or scope.

## Resolve dependencies and integrate findings

Connect each contribution to the parent proposition or artifact it completes. Sequence prerequisites; parallelize independent reads or isolated writes under TEAM. Use deterministic tools for settled transformations and retain economic judgment with its owner. Decompose only until the next operation has executable inputs and a completion condition.

Return a consequential mismatch to its producing owner and rebuild affected consumers. Reuse unaffected definitions and results; continue the original outstanding deliverable through the existing context, without a separate ledger.

## Interpret a consequential result

At a result determining the main claim, action or next research stage, compare the saved quantity, sample, units and specification with the adopted question, predicted mechanism and decision criterion. An expected result proceeds to its consumer. A mismatch identifies its producing formulation, evidence, method, implementation, calculation or interpretation and an observable repair condition. Scientific changes retain existing authority, budgets and convergence rules.

For machine-readable results, `research_design.py interpret CONTRACT --root PROJECT --output OUTPUT` binds exact `{path,sha256}` specification/result references and the owner's judgment to actual result-field observations. Record comparison, criterion, conclusion and `retain|repair|authority_required`; name the producing owner and next operation for a mismatch. Bind that saved record to its artifact and, on resumed selection, `research_decisions.interpretation={path,sha256}`. Changed inputs invalidate reuse. An explanation without saved analysis uses its existing draft/source correspondence.

After a targeted repair, resolve the mismatch from its returned evidence. Continue only for a concrete remaining defect with an authorized resolving operation. Use the existing decision/task context for recovery; PROSE governs expression.

## Operational plans

For a requested plan, specify responsible actions, inputs, handoffs, cooperation, update triggers and completion conditions within adopted time and resources. Derive acceptance from the requested target; distinguish reaching a level from improving a baseline. Preserve required frequencies and existing routines. Add a record, check or meeting only for an actual decision, recovery need or user requirement.
