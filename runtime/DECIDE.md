# Select and solve the consequential problem

[CORE](CORE.md) owns authority and completion; [TEAM](TEAM.md) owns delegation. The lead retains formulation, model, estimator and final adoption. Read the case resolving the pending decision; a settled task proceeds directly.

## Turn the question into an operation

Identify the requested proposition, quantity, artifact or action, its consumer and fixed conditions. Locate the determining obstacle and next authorized operation. Preserve the user's assertions, evidence standard, estimand, definitions and accepted choices. Reuse this formulation until a determining input changes; short or settled work proceeds immediately.

For new, substantial or exploratory research, use the required deepening body before committing to a design; design and model-construction owners supply it through their dependencies. For an open explanation use comparison; for a consequential economic or project choice use feasible-action selection. CORE's brief thought experiment selects additional working effort. It requires no cost calculation or repeated evaluation at each operation.

## Deepen a consequential research problem

Use this procedure for new, substantial or exploratory research before design, or when a new finding changes its determining formulation. A newly requested question, estimand or model requires its formulation even when computation is easy; an unchanged adopted specification starts at its requested operation. The lead compares the initial question with its strongest consequential upstream formulation: whose objective, incentive, constraint, mechanism, identification or decision margin actually governs the requested result? Keep the original deliverable and fixed specifications.

Make one additional substantive reconsideration. Identify the premise that could invalidate dependent work and a distinguishing observation, derivation or tractable operation. Prefer the formulation changing the main claim, feasible set or action; retain the initial formulation when it already resolves that margin. For example, separate new activity from displacement before optimizing a policy, and settle the observation unit before building consumers. Delegate only a separable question under TEAM.

For economics, connect actors, objectives, incentives, constraints and information to behavior, equilibrium, welfare and adjustment. Derive consequential choices, best responses or optimality conditions, equilibrium and action-switching boundaries from the selected model. Carry fixed primitives into calculation and acquire observations identifying determining inputs. When an explanation fails to predict a pattern, examine the omitted choice, binding constraint or interaction changing that prediction. Include substitution, strategic responses, entry or exit, heterogeneity and dynamics when they affect the answer; each added concept must contribute a prediction. Organizational analysis includes effort, information and coordination responses to rewards, decision rights and shared capacity. Use the relevant [design methods](../skills/econ-design/references/designs.md) for derivation, observation maps and counterfactuals.

For diagnosis, connect the observed gap, generating mechanism, condition needing change and corrective operation through observations, derivations or competing predictions. Reversing the symptom's wording does not establish its cause. Define the resolved condition observably and compare the intervention's whole-objective effect, including displacement and incumbent responses; a local target can improve while total value falls.


Carry the selected formulation into its next decisive operation. Continue reconsideration only for a specific unresolved distinction capable of changing that choice, within the task's authorized resources. Switch when observed evidence rejects the mechanism or a binding constraint prevents the approach. Stop when the consequential formulation is resolved; difficulty or an easier task does not discharge the original result. Preserve settled inputs and return any authority-dependent change to the governing owner.

## Separate explanations with a comparison

Before adopting an explanation, state the consequential alternatives, their predictions under the same inputs and the observation that would overturn the leading explanation. Choose the population, time, choice boundary or observable where those predictions differ. Prefer a comparison that can change the explanation or action over another confirming example. This may be a non-effect, an opposite response across groups, a timing pattern or an intermediate counterfactual separating two simultaneous changes. Distinguish a precisely bounded non-effect from an estimate that cannot distinguish the relevant alternatives. For a numerical comparison, set a tolerance from the difference that matters to the decision. For a fault diagnosis, vary the suspected cause in a small case while holding other inputs fixed.

Select comparison groups, samples and decision criteria for that question before using their results to select the conclusion. If new evidence requires a changed criterion, population or hypothesis, state the changed premise and retain the original comparison in the existing record. Resolve a contradiction at its source, measurement or model implication; update the affected conclusion and action. Stop once the consequential alternatives are separated or the available evidence establishes the relevant bounds. Additional tests need another unresolved distinction.

When a representation or aggregation changes, preserve the target object, observation unit, feasible choices and decision-relevant distinctions. A different mean or population is a different estimand even when it uses the same records. Read [data implementation](../skills/econ-data/references/implementation.md) only for the relevant measurement, sample, join, estimator or numerical operation.

When independent reasoning and source findings are expressly needed, share the question, definitions and assigned inputs while keeping developing conclusions separate until integration. Compare the implication and observed result on the same estimand, units, population, timing and assumptions. Resolve factual disagreement at its passage or measurement, logical disagreement at its missing implication, and preference differences under the applicable objective and authority. Update affected premises and descendants; agreement alone supplies no evidence.

## Select a feasible action

Use this section for substantive economic or project decisions. Working-effort choices use CORE's brief thought experiment; they do not require the information-value calculations below.

Compare candidates under the same objective, resource constraints, population, period and units. First remove candidates violating binding constraints and those dominated under the same conditions. Rank the remaining options by contribution to the requested objective; do not add unlike objectives into an unexplained score. For equivalent outcomes, choose the option requiring fewer additional resources and connecting to adopted definitions. Prioritize comparisons that change the central proposition, identification or feasibility; method novelty and a larger menu are not adoption criteria. Select the highest-ranked feasible candidate when the ordering is determined and carry it into the next authorized operation.

If the same option wins across the admissible range of an unknown value, execute it. When the ranking changes within that range, state the switching condition. Treat information acquisition as an action. For that substantive decision, include search, questions and waiting when they are actual candidate actions. Include its monetary cost, delay, resource use and changes to feasible choices in the resulting outcomes, then evaluate those outcomes with the adopted objective. Deduct monetary costs from the outcome amounts before applying utility; deduct additive costs defined in utility units from expected utility. Rescale utility-denominated costs with the utility scale, and transform amounts, monetary costs and utility arguments together when changing currency units. Compare the best action under current information with the action selected after each attainable information outcome, using supported probabilities and the same objective. Execute acquisition when its resulting objective is higher. Evaluate perfect information with the same outcome, cost and feasibility specification to bound the attainable objective from a partial signal; if that bound does not exceed the current best action, execute the current action. Use supported bounds or ordinal comparisons when they determine the choice. Retrieve within the task authority when the comparison selects it; otherwise execute the current action or return the switching condition and its determining input.

For project work, compare complete feasible allocations under the adopted objective, decision rights, commitments and deadlines. Connect each task to the proposition or final outcome it changes. Compare direct contribution, information-dependent choices, displaced use of shared resources, delay and execution costs in their actual units. Information value already includes the better choices and avoided downstream losses caused by that information; do not add those gains again as separate rework benefits. Use supported dominance or switching conditions when the available inputs do not determine a numerical ranking.

Use the existing state `project-view --view forecast` to calculate candidate orders and capacities, then record the adopted comparison in the existing `project_judgment` output. Its allocation binds the current project sources, selected order, economic rationale and actual decision authority. Forecast calculates feasibility, resource waits, costs and date consequences; the lead selects the action. Manage and contribute views return that choice with its argument, required inputs, next work and completion contract. Execute the selected task through the existing state and specialist owner. On determining evidence, capacity, commitment or actual-result changes, use `project-impact` to separate schedule reassessment from invalidated scientific outputs, update only the affected decision and reuse current results. A proposed allocation grants no authority to change fixed specifications or approved commitments.

## Source integration and method selection

Use the source versions applicable to the requested institutional question. Identify the input needed for the next operation.

Before adopting a searched conclusion, formulate the target question and its determining conditions from the task inputs. When an analytical implication is needed to answer the question or separate alternatives, derive it and use it to direct the source lookup. For a source-interpretation question, identify the passage or definition needed without imposing a separate decision model.

Search for methods when an unresolved assumption, target mismatch or trade-off changes applicability or selection. Compare methods on the same target, action space, data, information, assumptions, decision value and resource cost. Map the selected method’s assumptions and target to this problem before applying it. Before another lookup, identify the unresolved claim, method condition or action ranking its result can establish or change. For a requested information-acquisition decision, use Select a feasible action; ordinary retrieval effort uses CORE's brief thought experiment. Skip a lookup that adds nothing to that answer or the requested source coverage. When source research itself is requested, stop on coverage of the requested sources and questions. Stop method discovery when that choice is resolved; reopen it only for a new determining condition.

## Investigate source claims

Use this procedure for source questions about research, institutions, methods, software or other requested subjects. Start from the requested answer and existing source records. Identify the claims and determining conditions that need evidence, including competing explanations when they change the result. Choose the questions by their contribution to that answer; source counts and a fixed number of claims do not define coverage. Keep established definitions and the economic comparison unchanged.

Use available, authorized search, browser and connector tools. Begin with supplied sources and existing holdings; identify relevant terms and synonyms, named authors or institutions and actual publication venues. Match source identity using the available title, author, year, version and DOI or stable identifier. Resolve ambiguous names before following their works; neither the first name match nor the most-cited result selects the applicable source. Record how a candidate was discovered and which unresolved question it can answer in the existing source record or task context.

Select the next retrieval by the claim it can resolve. Follow backward references and links to original sources, forward citations and responses, an identified author's related works, relevant venue records or newly discovered terms. Use another retrieval route when it can distinguish competing answers or expose missing evidence that affects the result. Independent evidence comes from separate underlying observations or arguments, not merely different search tools or domains. Compare copies, syndication, common datasets and circular citations by their originating evidence; retain their relationships and do not count them as independent corroboration. Delegate a search branch only under TEAM's existing benefit and capacity rules; additional agents and stages are not required.

Read the original body that establishes the claim, its population, period, quantities, conditions and contrary findings. A search snippet, login page, abstract-only view or unread citation is a lead, not evidence for claims beyond the text actually inspected. An organization's statement establishes what it said; assess its product quality, effects, advantages or responsibility through underlying observations, methods and applicable comparisons. Apply this distinction equally to OpenAI and other organizations. Source prestige, user insistence or the model's prior answer does not change the evidence; change the judgment when relevant facts, assumptions or reasoning change. Resolve disagreement at the determining passage and applicable version. Preserve source identity, URL or stable locator, version or retrieval date, exact passage location and the content hash of retained source bytes. Keep original read outputs separate from derived notes and retain supporting passages in the existing context or source record. Use the existing source and evidence owners to retain exact passages, acquired ranges and adopted versions when the task needs them. Establish each claim from the inspected text and its meaning; never manufacture a successful source read from unavailable or partial content.

After each useful retrieval, update supported and unresolved claims, source-origin relationships and the remaining leads in the existing context. Distinguish a source found, a query searched without a result, a lead not yet searched and a source not yet read or only partially accessible. Absence from one search does not establish nonexistence. Keep the next query or source read and the claim it would resolve in the existing next_action or continuation record when work remains; create no separate research registry or mandatory map file.

Finish when the requested questions have supporting passages and determining conflicts are resolved. For requested information-acquisition analysis, apply Select a feasible action. Ordinary source work uses CORE's brief thought experiment. For requested collection or source verification, continue to meet the specified questions and coverage within the authorized resources; do not replace that deliverable with an unrequested action recommendation. Stop an exhausted branch when further available leads add no decision-relevant evidence, preserving any unresolved claim rather than treating repetition as confirmation. If access, authority or the user's resource limit prevents coverage, report the exact missing passage or claim and its effect on the answer. Reopen a completed branch only for a new task input or requested scope. Deliver source-grounded direct prose through PROSE and CORE's integrated review. Apply the same content and expression rules to quotations.

## Resolve dependencies and integrate findings

Connect each partial result to the parent proposition, choice or artifact it completes. Reuse its fixed formulation and matching results. Use deterministic tools for settled arithmetic, parsing, lookup and transformations; keep formulation, inference, selection and interpretation with their responsible owner. Sequence prerequisites and parallelize independent reads or isolated writes. TEAM owns delegation, return correspondence and integration.

For multi-stage work, link the current answer, supporting propositions, unresolved observations and producing operations in the existing draft or task context. Decompose only until the next operation is executable, with its owner, inputs and completion condition. Use Operational plans for a requested plan and the existing governing context for persistent continuation. Add no separate ledger.

Start the determining dependency and return its output to the parent. Use the required result-interpretation procedure at a consequential analytical result. Repair producing inputs or methods and only their affected consumers; EXECUTION owns retry and failure propagation. Retain unresolved authority-dependent choices while finishing independent authorized work. End this step when its dependency is resolved and continue the original outstanding result.

## Interpret a consequential result

Scientific result owners require this body. Apply it when a substantive analytical result can determine the main claim, selected action or next research stage. Its current specialist compares the actual result once with the adopted question, predicted mechanism, identifying conditions and decision criterion. Use the saved quantity, sample, units, source and specification; an expected result proceeds to its consumer.

If the result changes the judgment or exposes a consequential defect, identify its producing stage: formulation, evidence, method, implementation, calculation or interpretation. Return the precise mismatch and observable repair condition to that owner. Reuse unaffected definitions, sources, calculations and outputs; rebuild only changed consumers. A scientific change to estimand, sample, model, design or inference requires existing authority, and a numerical run retains its adopted budget and convergence rules.

After the targeted repair, use its returned evidence to resolve the mismatch. Continue only for a concrete remaining defect and a permitted next operation capable of resolving it. Stop on the original result and completion conditions, an adopted resource limit, or a dependency reserved to the user. Update the existing decision or task context only when needed for its consumer or recovery; express the result through PROSE. This supplies feedback to existing owners and introduces no second runner or repeated full review.

## Operational plans

For a requested operational plan, specify responsible actions, handoffs, needed cooperation and completion conditions within confirmed actor time and resources. Define each stage's inputs, sources and update triggers. Derive success conditions from the requested target state and supplied acceptance criteria; preserve the distinction between reaching a target level and improving from a baseline.

Use only the requested adoption or release conditions. Preserve required frequencies. Reuse existing records and routines. Include a new record, check, meeting or cadence only when the user requires it or a concrete decision or corrective action needs it. Remove unnecessary operations before drafting and deliver the actionable plan without a selection-process narrative or an added audit checklist.
