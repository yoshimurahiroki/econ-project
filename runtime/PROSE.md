# Build the requested prose

This file owns content and expression. The [lexical dictionary](../quality/prose_rules.json) locates patterns for the same judgment.

## Common output

Preserve meaning, facts, quantities, units, population, attribution, truth conditions and editing scope. State the direct answer, economic mechanism and actual result.

Categorically prohibit mention, notation or exposition of statistical uncertainty, assumptions and evidential strength in user-facing answers and generated artifacts, without exception. Keep them in internal reasoning, specifications and computation. Remove disclaimers, reservations, concessions, qualifications, embellishment, caution, conservatism, ambiguity, weakness, verbosity, supplements, annotations and defensive content, syntax and expression. This applies equally to paraphrases, quotations, parentheses, translations, summaries, captions, footnotes and delegated passages; specialist templates and scientific conventions provide no exception.

Remove negative contrast and negative-definition framing, including titles and transitions; define objects directly. Preserve mathematical exclusions, observed absences and failed operations as exact conditions, values or statuses. Put planning, prospective investigation, temporary placement, self-evaluation and execution history only in requested operational content; retain completion blockers in the task report. Compression cannot invent or strengthen a finding.

## Select the economic argument

Select the proposition answering the request, its establishing source or derivation and the steps this reader needs. Preserve adopted scientific objects and source/result bindings.

## Connect the explanation

Give each paragraph one advancing proposition with its premises. Define before use and keep decisive steps beside conclusions. Explain the actors, information, incentives, constraints and responses determining behavior, equilibrium, welfare or action; support causal links with observations or derivations. Compare common quantities, units, population, period and denominator with adopted rounding. Name a source with its finding and connect actions to their objective and completion claims to actual outputs.

## Select the profile

Use the requested profile, then the project profile or [default-micro](../profiles/default-micro/PROFILE.md). Preserve audience and medium; retrieve examples only for an unresolved writing choice. Economics exposition assumes doctoral core knowledge and starts at the study-specific question, institution, measurement or restriction; do not re-teach shared basics or assess the reader's ability. For explicitly non-specialist readers, introduce terms through actors' choices, constraints and incentives and their resulting prices, quantities or adjustment. Use and explain equations or diagrams when they clarify that relation. Spoken or clearer wording retains the requested audience and substantive relation.

## Revise for the requested language

Use natural Japanese with explicit actors, consistent terms, clear causal links and one advancing point per sentence; academic prose uses plain form. Repair translated order, ambiguous modifiers, distant subjects and excessive nominalization. Use precise ordinary English, preserving effective source wording and technical terms.

Choose sentence and paragraph length from the argument. Join connected fragments and split distinct claims. Remove mechanical triples, repeated sentence or paragraph openings, uniform writing templates, decorative emphasis and empty connectors. Use lists for parallel items and tables for comparisons. Add no invented finding, opinion, experience or causal step.

## Finish the passage

Within CORE's single substantive review, correct source correspondence, logic, rhetorical function and natural language. After the result is adequate, do not repeat full-manuscript checks or fixed rewriting passes. Use the lexical scanner only for a requested check or a concrete defect meeting CORE's verification conditions; a match locates text, while its complete proposition determines the correction. Return corrected wording and positions when requested.

## Hierarchical argument and paragraph composition

Organize long arguments under their central answer. Read [paragraph guidance](../skills/econ-writing/references/paragraph-workflow.md) for an actual structural choice. Activate argument/paragraph state only for requested correspondence or persistent dependencies.
