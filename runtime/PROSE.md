## Select the economic argument

Use the established reader, purpose, medium, central question and permitted content changes. Lead with the content the reader needs to understand, judge or act on. Select content by its contribution to the requested answer; delete generic background, importance claims and empty framing. Connect a necessary general principle to what it explains here.

Before drafting, identify the contribution each paragraph makes to the requested answer: a claim, evidence, definition, derivation, comparison, mechanism or execution condition as needed. These are possible functions, not a required sequence or checklist; a function can occupy a clause, paragraph or section according to the argument. Give it the question or claim that function serves. Remove disclaimers, reservations, concessions, rhetorical qualifications, ornamental praise, cautious or conservative posture, supplementary or annotative framing, vague hedging, weak assertions and defensive framing without exceptions. Remove supplements, annotations, footnotes and postscripts that qualify or protect the claim. Moving a prohibited function to another location does not resolve it. At each lexical hit, assess its actual function, sense, syntax and target proposition; remove prohibited functions. Order sentences by the relation needed to establish the claim: premise to implication, institution to choice, choice to outcome, or comparison to decision. Carry established information into the next claim instead of repeating it. Paragraph count and sentence order follow the argument.

For a contrast, ask whether removing either side changes the conclusion, reason, choice or meaning of a quantity. Remove the side that does no such work and state the remaining proposition directly. Express comparisons that distinguish explanations, change a ranking or establish the result's magnitude and population in direct wording. Judge each matched occurrence by its actual sense, syntactic role, proposition and surrounding function; a comparison or logical label alone is insufficient. Compress estimates that establish the same claim. In successive English comparisons, state the first comparison and result before introducing the distinction tested next; put that next design and its estimates where the distinction arises. A change of inferential purpose determines the next unit of explanation. Explain a changed specification or assumption when it changes the claim or its conditions.

For a decision, state the feasible choice or switching condition under the adopted objective and constraints, using the premises and calculations that determine the ranking. Include a scenario calculation when requested or when it can change that choice. For unresolved option selection use [DECIDE](DECIDE.md), then return to the passage.

## Connect the explanation

Use the same term for the same object. Name the intended referent when a subject, pronoun or quantity is ambiguous. Introduce a concept through the behavior, comparison or quantity it names. Retain definitions that recover the mechanism or comparison; repeat one only when its referent has become unclear. In English, repair nominalizations and participial clauses that hide the actor, action or relation between claims, and parallel phrases that repeat the same information. Keep referents and number consistent. In Japanese, repair unexplained topic shifts, unclear demonstratives, noun chains and modifiers detached from their predicates. Choose predicates that state the relation and name an actor when omission changes who acts. Replace repetitive predicates when they obscure distinct relations. Diagnose these constructions by the information they obscure, not their frequency.

Connect sentences using supported cause and result, derivation, addition, comparison, specification, example or transition to a new question. Use a connective or bridging clause when the relation would otherwise be unclear; use established referents and sentence order when they already convey it. A causal connective requires a supported causal relation. For an established information or incentive mechanism, connect the information or incentive to the choice it changes and then to the outcome. Apply the same relation test to slide headings, bullets and equations together.

When a compound definition obscures whether conditions hold jointly or alternatively, give the requirements separate clauses to make the structure readable. Resolve every dictionary hit by repair or a candidate-bound occurrence judgment covering its sense, syntax, proposition and original-source identity. Source quotations and bibliography receive the same functional review.
