## Select the economic argument

Read the requested text, determining sources and adopted specification. Select the central proposition that changes the reader's understanding or action, its supporting evidence or derivation, and the explanatory steps that make it recoverable. Resolve a consequential missing calculation or failed operation through its owner before polishing. Preserve facts, quantities, attribution, truth conditions, authority and editing scope.

Retain the direct answer, economic mechanism, result and authorized operation. Categorically prohibit mention, notation or exposition of statistical uncertainty, assumptions and evidential strength in user-facing answers and generated artifacts, without exception. Keep them in internal reasoning, specifications and computation. Remove disclaimers, reservations, concessions, qualifications, embellishment, caution, conservatism, ambiguity, weakness, verbosity, supplements, annotations and defensive content, syntax and expression. Apply the same prohibition to paraphrases, quotations, parentheses, footnotes, captions, translations, summaries and delegated passages. A specialist's reporting template or scientific convention supplies no output exception.

Remove negative contrast and negative-definition framing. Define the object directly and name each finding's actor, operation, population and quantity. Apply this rule to titles, transitions, captions, translations, reviewer responses and examples. Preserve mathematical exclusions, observed absences and failed operations through their exact conditions, values and returned statuses.

Research exposition presents the adopted economic argument and completed results. Keep planning commentary, temporary-placement language, prospective investigation, writing intentions and self-evaluation in an expressly requested operational artifact. Under CORE's record contract, retain source gaps and failed operations in the owning record and dependent completion report. Statistical diagnostics, identifying conditions and source-strength judgments govern internal selection and calculation acceptance. Deliver the economic answer and actual operation status directly; omission or compression cannot invent a finding, strengthen a claim or alter its population, quantities and units.

## Connect the explanation

Give each paragraph one advancing proposition and its establishing premises. Put definitions before use and decisive steps beside the conclusion. State actors, information, constraints, incentives and responses when they determine behavior, equilibrium, welfare or action. Support causal links with observations or derivations.

Compare actual objects using common units, population, period, denominator and the determining condition. Apply adopted rounding. Name the source and its finding. Connect a selected action to the requested objective and actual evidence. Match reported completion to saved outputs and returned writes.

## Select the profile

Use the requested profile, then the adopted project profile or [default-micro](../profiles/default-micro/PROFILE.md). Read an example only to resolve a concrete writing choice. Preserve the requested audience and medium.

Select content from what this reader or listener already knows. Omit self-evident definitions, textbook premises, routine methodological explanations and repeated descriptions of the exhibit. Start at the study-specific fact, mechanism or inference the audience needs to reach the answer. Preserve the decisive logical connection without re-teaching its shared prerequisites. Apply this selection to spoken explanations, slides, captions, notes and summaries as well as prose; do not announce the omitted basics or describe the audience's level in the output.

For economics prose, assume doctoral core knowledge of microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics, games, optimization, equilibrium and causal inference. Start with the study's economic question. Explain institutions, specialized concepts, restrictions and measurement where they determine the argument. Clearer or spoken wording changes expression while retaining that audience; omit assessments of the reader's ability.

For explicitly non-specialist readers, begin with actors, available choices, constraints and changing incentives. Follow responses through prices, quantities and adjustment. Introduce each term through that relation. Use an equation or diagram when it makes an inference visible, and explain its choices and movement in ordinary language. Preserve the actual question, conditions, comparison and result.

Author profile rules and examples from specified settings and source-bound operations. Keep illustrative inputs with their example and derive every displayed quantity from them. Compose original guidance from inspected public writing. Preserve required third-party notices and keep private source identifiers in private records.

## Revise for the requested language

Write natural Japanese with explicit actors, consistent terms, clear causal links and one main proposition per sentence. Repair translated word order, ambiguous modification, distant subjects, excessive nominalization and unnatural inanimate agency. Use plain-form Japanese for academic prose. In English, use precise ordinary words and direct verbs. Preserve effective source wording and established technical terms.

Choose sentence and paragraph length from the argument. Join fragments establishing one relation; split distinct claims. Remove mechanical triples, repeated openings, uniform templates, decorative emphasis and empty connectors. Use lists for parallel items and tables for comparisons. Add no invented finding, source, experience, opinion or causal step.
