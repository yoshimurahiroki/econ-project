# Structural paper evidence

Read the [common paper-reading procedure](paper-reading.md) and this complete method procedure. Combine method procedures when the requested argument uses them together.

## Read a structural paper

1. **Output: structural question and identification; consumer: estimation reading.** Populate the common five-part map with the economic model, information, timing, normalization and equilibrium conditions. Connect observed variation or moments to identification and then to the likelihood, moment conditions or estimation objective. Record estimated, calibrated and fixed quantities with their supporting observations and roles in answering the research question.
2. **Output: inspected fit and counterfactual; consumer: economic judgment.** Follow estimation and numerical solution into baseline predictions and fit. Read each intervention with its invariant primitives, changed equilibrium, population, units and horizon. Follow welfare to its value function, aggregation and scenario-specific quantities. Read determining appendix or implementation definitions for solvers, integration, parameter restrictions and equilibrium selection.
3. **Output: supported implication and contribution; consumer: five-part record.** Connect the fitted mechanism and counterfactual to the important economic question and the inspected literature comparison. Retain assumptions, diagnostics and numerical conditions internally. Pass the exact implementation function, version and inputs to econ-data for authorized calculation.
