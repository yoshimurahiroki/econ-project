# Structural paper evidence

Read the [common paper-reading procedure](paper-reading.md) and this complete method procedure. Combine method procedures when the requested argument uses them together.

## Read a structural paper

Read the economic model, information and timing, normalization and equilibrium conditions before interpreting fitted parameters. Connect observed variation or moments to identification and then to the likelihood, moment conditions or other estimation objective. Identify estimated, calibrated and fixed quantities and the observations supporting each role.

Follow the estimation and numerical solution into the model's baseline predictions and fit. Read the intervention, invariant primitives, changed equilibrium, population, units and horizon for each requested counterfactual. Follow welfare to its value function, aggregation and scenario-specific quantities. Read the appendix or replication implementation when solver definitions, integration, parameter restrictions or equilibrium selection determine the result. Pass its exact function, version and inputs to econ-data for an authorized calculation.
