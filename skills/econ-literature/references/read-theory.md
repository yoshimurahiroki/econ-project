# Theory paper checklist

Use with the [paper-reading sheet](paper-reading.md).

```
Agents, objectives, feasible choices, information, timing:
Equilibrium concept and selection rule:
Primitives and maintained restrictions:
Main proposition:      statement with domain and quantifiers; existence and uniqueness conditions
Proof:                 determining lemmas and steps; boundary cases and counterexamples
Comparative statics:   through feasible sets, active constraints and equilibrium responses
Welfare:               objective, population and aggregation
Mechanism the derivation establishes, and its answer to the question:
Advance over prior results:
```
