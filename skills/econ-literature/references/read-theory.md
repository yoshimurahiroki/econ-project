# Theory paper evidence

Read the [common paper-reading procedure](paper-reading.md) and this complete method procedure. Combine method procedures when the requested argument uses them together.

## Read a theory paper

1. **Output: theoretical question and model; consumer: proof reading.** Populate the common five-part map with agents, objectives, feasible choices, information, timing and equilibrium concept. Recover the primitives and maintained restrictions used by the proposition; connect its economic object to the important research question.
2. **Output: inspected proposition and derivation; consumer: mechanism judgment.** Read the statement's domain, quantifiers, existence/uniqueness conditions and selection rule. Follow each determining lemma, proof step and boundary counterexample. Retrieve other propositions supplying actual dependencies. Trace comparative statics through feasible sets, active constraints and equilibrium responses; trace welfare through the objective, population and aggregation.
3. **Output: supported implication and contribution; consumer: five-part record.** Explain the mechanism established by the derivation and its answer to the original question. Bind the advance over prior results to the inspected proposition and comparison; retain restrictions and proof dependencies internally.
