# Theory paper evidence

Read the [common paper-reading procedure](paper-reading.md) and this complete method procedure. Combine method procedures when the requested argument uses them together.

## Read a theory paper

Identify agents, objectives, feasible choices, information, timing and equilibrium concept. Recover every primitive and maintained restriction used by the requested proposition. Read its statement with the domain, quantifiers, existence or uniqueness conditions and any selection rule. Follow each determining lemma and proof step, including a counterexample used to establish a boundary.

Trace a comparative static through the feasible set, active constraints and equilibrium responses. Trace welfare through the stated objective, population and aggregation. Explain the economic mechanism established by the derivation, then connect that implication to the original question. Select the proof and prerequisites of the requested proposition; retrieve another proposition when its content supplies an actual dependency.
