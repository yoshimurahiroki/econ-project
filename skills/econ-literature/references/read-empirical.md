# Empirical paper evidence

Read the [common paper-reading procedure](paper-reading.md) and this complete method procedure. Combine method procedures when the requested argument uses them together.

## Read an empirical paper

Recover the intervention, target population, estimand and source of identifying variation before interpreting its coefficients. Follow the observation, assignment, analysis and inference units into the sample and equation. Read treatment timing, comparison-group construction, weighting, fixed effects and the assumptions that identify the stated parameter. Follow a result's transformations and aggregation into its reported units.

Read the main estimate with its standard error or interval, reference distribution, table notes and the contrast it estimates. Read diagnostics, alternative specifications, heterogeneous effects or additional outcomes when they distinguish the paper's mechanism or change its central conclusion. Connect the paper's observed contrast to its causal interpretation through the stated identifying restrictions. Retain the source's actual evidence and quantities when explaining the contribution.
