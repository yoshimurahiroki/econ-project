## Follow the argument to its evidence

First recover the question, central answer, contribution and section structure from the introduction, abstract, conclusion and named tables or propositions. Use that orientation to locate the model, identifying comparison, result and argument required by the requested output. Screening an abstract establishes only what that abstract states.

Read each selected section through the reasoning that supports its result. Follow definitions, assumptions, equations, table notes, figure captions, proofs and appendix references when they determine that reasoning. For a reported estimate, read its population, sample, outcome definition, comparison, estimator, inference and actual reported value together. For a theoretical proposition, read its domain, quantifiers and proof dependencies together. Continue along a cited dependency until the selected claim is supported by the inspected original material.

Select the paper's method procedure: [empirical](read-empirical.md) (`paper_empirical`), [theory](read-theory.md) (`paper_theory`) or [structural](read-structural.md) (`paper_structural`). Read the selected file through the end. Combine procedures when the requested argument uses those methods together. An exact passage check begins at its locator and follows its defining context. A method-implementation task also reads the matching author code or official package interface. Preserve an existing research design and saved results when explaining the source.

For an additional literature search, acquire new evidence through the shared search-priority rule and its execution-year windows. For an already assigned paper, continue from that paper and its determining source dependencies. Keep discovery, reading and interpretation attached to the same study identity.

## Retain the inspected source and hand it forward

Keep the original PDF, HTML or text in the research project's existing source storage. Preserve one bibliographic study identity across its explicitly reconciled editions. Store extracted page text, selected propositions, relations, reading observations and uses through the existing source record described in [source discovery](source-discovery.md). A stored title, extracted document or search hit supplies no observation that its body was read.

Retrieve selected document IDs, pages or proposition IDs for the pending claim. Return the exact edition, source and page hashes, section or equation/table locator, the inspected text and the source-supported proposition. Record an AI reading observation only for text actually delivered and inspected. Record a human reading assertion only from the researcher's supplied declaration. Keep source assertions, estimates, assumptions and the task owner's derived interpretation attributable to their actual actors.

Pass the selected source to econ-design for an adopted model or identifying comparison, econ-data for the authorized calculation, econ-review for the requested assessment and econ-writing:explain for the completed explanation. Carry the selected paper's venue, publication status, dates and research-selection reason through that handoff. A changed edition updates its affected claims and uses through the existing record; reuse unchanged calculations and passages.
