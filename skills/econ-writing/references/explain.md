# Explain economics prose

Use the [shared writing contract](../SKILL.md) and read this complete mode before its operation.

## Explain

Explain the specified paper, theory, institution, method or result at the requested depth. Select the relevant question and answer, then develop the source-supported mechanism and evidence through the common process.

For a paper, activate `paper_reading` and its actual method condition: `paper_empirical`, `paper_theory` or `paper_structural`. Read [paper reading](../../econ-literature/references/paper-reading.md) and its selected method file through the end. Use `econ-literature:investigate` with `research_literature_search` when the requested explanation also needs new paper selection or prior-research comparison. Carry that selection's venue, publication status and execution-year priority into the completed explanation.

### Read and select the argument

Read the cited passage and the supporting material needed to understand its objects, premises, operations and result. Choose the information the reader needs to recover the answer. Use the requested audience or the adopted profile to decide which concepts need explanation.

### Develop the connected explanation

Connect actors, rules, choices, causal or theoretical mechanisms, identifying comparisons and numerical magnitudes in their logical order. Show the equation or comparison that carries the selected result into the paper's answer, using its actual population, conditions and valuation. For Japanese, form the complete explanation a reader can follow aloud, separate distinct topics, and then compress the established argument. Match the explanation to the source's actual result.

### Complete the requested explanation

Complete the integrated review of the final explanation, including its source correspondence and causal links. Return the answer and its decisive evidence at the requested depth.
