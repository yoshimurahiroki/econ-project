# Explain economics prose

## Explain

Apply [PROSE Common output](../../../runtime/PROSE.md#common-output) to every element.

Explain the specified paper, theory, institution, method or result at the requested depth, using source-supported mechanism and evidence.

For a paper, activate `paper_reading` and its actual method condition: `paper_empirical`, `paper_theory` or `paper_structural`. Read [paper reading](../../econ-literature/references/paper-reading.md) and its selected method file through the end. Use `econ-literature:investigate` with `research_literature_search` when the requested explanation also needs new paper selection or prior-research comparison. Carry that selection's venue, publication status and execution-year priority into the completed explanation.

### Read and select the argument

Read the cited passage and supporting material needed to understand its objects, premises, operations and result. Find and read eligible originals for the information and expression needed by the requested audience or adopted profile.

### Develop the connected explanation

Borrow corresponding source structures and expressions for actors, rules, choices, mechanisms, identifying comparisons and magnitudes. Keep the actual population, conditions and valuation in the internal source record; select supported external claims under PROSE. Apply [PROSE's language rules](../../../runtime/PROSE.md#revise-for-the-requested-language) and match the explanation to the actual result.

### Complete the requested explanation

Complete the integrated review of the final explanation, including source correspondence and causal links. Compare every element with its mapped original and return the answer and decisive evidence at the requested depth.
