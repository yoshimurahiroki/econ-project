# Explain economics prose

## Explain

1. For a paper, read it through [paper reading](../../econ-literature/references/paper-reading.md) and the method file for its type (empirical, theory, structural).
2. Answer in five points: question, why it matters, method and identifying comparison, results with magnitudes, contribution. Use the actual population and units of the paper.
3. For a theory, institution or method, follow steps 2 to 4 of the [economist's procedure](../../../runtime/ECONOMIST.md): actors and incentives, mechanism and predictions, the comparison that tests them.
4. Write under [WRITING](../../../runtime/WRITING.md); Japanese explanations take their expression from Japanese lecture exemplars.
