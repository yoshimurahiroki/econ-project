# Paragraph and argument execution

## Required writing path

Use [econ-writing's common process](../SKILL.md#compose-for-the-medium-and-language) to complete the requested argument and its integrated editorial review. The labels A–G retain the existing formal interface. In ordinary writing, A–F describe reasoning and integration performed in the draft; they create no extra agents, passes, records or gates. Use the parts that resolve the actual dependencies of a long argument.

When the user explicitly selects a formal paragraph certification or the applicable platform contract requires it, execute the existing [CONTROL](../../../runtime/CONTROL.md) contract, including its source-linked state, actual host assignments and required review records. The selected formal path remains executable and retains its completion requirements.

## A. Global Argument Architect

Identify the reader's question, the central answer, its premises and evidence. Work through the dependency from each supporting claim to the answer and select the order in which a reader can follow it. Build that explanation before shortening the draft.

## B. Paragraph Specification Designer

Give each necessary paragraph one central proposition and the reader question it answers. Decide which evidence or mechanism establishes that proposition and how it connects to the surrounding paragraphs. Allocate space by the work each step performs. Express these choices in the ordinary draft; a selected formal workflow uses its existing paragraph specifications.

## C. Paragraph Writers

Write a coherent paragraph that establishes its assigned claim and connects with neighboring paragraphs. For Japanese, reconstruct the complete explanation aloud in natural wording before compression. Preserve source-supported actors, operations, quantities and relations while applying PROSE's language rules.

## D. Local Quality Control

Resolve the local claim, source correspondence and wording as part of composition. The completed text's integrated review includes each paragraph's meaning, mechanism and expression. A separate automated check follows CORE's three-condition rule; a selected formal workflow uses its required local checks.

## E. Section Integration

Arrange the paragraphs so their concrete claims establish the section's proposition. Connect the steps, place definitions before use and preserve citations. Remove repeated or empty paragraphs and restore a missing premise from the source or completed analysis.

## F. Global Integration

Perform the one substantive integrated editorial review on the finished long argument. Assess the central answer, source support, logical order, section and paragraph connections, prohibited rhetoric and natural language together. Reconcile the title, opening, exhibits and body. Repair each defect found and reread the affected passages with their connections until complete.

## G. Independent final review and release

Use independent reviewers and the existing release checks when formal review is explicitly selected or required by the applicable platform contract. Complete its actual assignments and records under CONTROL. The ordinary writing path finishes through F and the requested artifact's existing delivery path.

## State, changes and resumption

Use the existing project record when interrupted work needs a durable handoff. Preserve source identity, settled choices, completed work and the next dependency. A selected formal workflow maintains its required state; ordinary writing creates no parallel paragraph ledger.
