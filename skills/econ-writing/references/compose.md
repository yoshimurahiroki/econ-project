# Compose economics prose

## Compose

1. Fill the [worksheet](../../../runtime/ECONOMIST.md) through step 6 first; the text expresses its question, mechanism, comparison, estimates and decision.
2. Take the section's argument from an English original: `exemplars.py outline --lang en --function F --query TOPIC` ([WRITING](../../../runtime/WRITING.md#1-borrow-the-argument-from-english-originals)). Long papers follow the section order in [paper guidance](paper-workflow.md#w3-compose-section-by-section).
3. Write in the requested language under [WRITING](../../../runtime/WRITING.md). Japanese takes its expression from Japanese exemplars (`find` and `style`), sentence by sentence.
4. Numbers, tables and figures come from saved results through the [result artifacts](result-artifacts.md) producers; a changed estimate regenerates every text and exhibit that uses it.
5. Render, read the output and finish under [WRITING](../../../runtime/WRITING.md#5-finish).
