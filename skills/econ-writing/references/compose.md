## Compose

Generate numerical passages, tables and figures from the same selected saved result. The [research renderer](../../../harness/research.md) binds each numerical placeholder to a consumed result's semantic key, exact unit and display format, retaining the producing input, definition, code and execution versions in the existing state record. Use the existing code to retrieve selected quantities, definitions and source identities. Use compose or revise to develop their economic claim into completed sentences through the [paragraph's evidence and inference](paragraph-workflow.md#compose-the-paragraph), retaining passage-level source correspondence. A changed estimate or defining assumption updates its affected numerical text and argument; a display-label change rebuilds its display consumers while reusing the calculation. Complete the substantive editorial review on the assembled paper and inspect the final artifact.

For a requested source/result correspondence map, use the [evidence binder](../../../harness/research_evidence.py) on the selected manuscript version. Bind claim-bearing passages to original source spans, displayed statistics to named result keys and units, and model equations and symbols to their actual code, source construction or calibrated specification and saved residuals. Retain the owner's substantive judgments with those exact identities. Select numerical coverage over the requested passage and account for source quantities, assumptions and citation years through their source-bound claims. Put the supported economic argument in the paper and its execution links in the existing result record.

For requested claim coverage or referee revision, use the [same evidence contract](result-artifacts.md#claims-coverage-and-requested-re-review) for material claim candidates, multiple evidence roles and fixed-criterion re-review. Follow the [manuscript-to-response sequence](paper-workflow.md#appendix-submission-and-referee-response): revise the actual affected passages, assess their current evidence, then compose the concrete response from that revision. Preserve the earlier manuscript and review; reuse judgments and calculations with unchanged claim dependencies. These selected operations add no independent review stage to ordinary composition.

Select the question, answer and decisive evidence from the established study or source materials. Give prominence to results that change the mechanism, action ranking, allocation or economic magnitude. Keep non-effects, subgroup contrasts and behavioral responses in the source record. Select external claims under PROSE. Recover a diagnostic's tested contrast and a model result's optimizing objective through [paper guidance](paper-workflow.md#compose-the-requested-section), then establish the inference carrying the central answer. Use [selected saved evidence](paragraph-workflow.md#command-entry-points) when that inference consumes a result outside the named statistics. Build the explanatory relation before drafting a compressed Abstract, slide or summary. A whole paper uses the same guidance for its section architecture.
