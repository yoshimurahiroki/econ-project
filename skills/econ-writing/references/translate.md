# Translate economics prose

## Translate

Apply [PROSE Common output](../../../runtime/PROSE.md#common-output) to every element.

Recover the source's claim, actor, mechanism and evidence. Search for and read corresponding eligible originals in the target language before selecting sentences, boundaries or order; borrow their expressions and structure with only the necessary adjustments. For Japanese, use Japanese originals rather than creating a direct translation of English prose. Apply [PROSE's language rules](../../../runtime/PROSE.md#revise-for-the-requested-language), preserving established technical terms, requested register, meaning-defining conditions and comparisons, quantities, attribution and claim strength. Retain the complete source record and apply PROSE with translation fidelity. Complete the common review against both the source being translated and every mapped original.
