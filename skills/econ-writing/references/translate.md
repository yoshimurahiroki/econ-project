# Translate economics prose

## Translate

Recover the source's claim, actor, mechanism and evidence before choosing target-language sentences. Use natural Japanese or English grammar, established technical terms and the requested register. Rebuild sentence boundaries and explanatory order where the source language's structure impedes comprehension. Preserve quantities, source attribution and supported findings. Keep assumptions and comparisons in the source record and select external claims under PROSE. Complete the common review against the source and final translation.
