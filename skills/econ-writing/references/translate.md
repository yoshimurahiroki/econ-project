# Translate economics prose

## Translate

1. Recover the claim, actors, mechanism and evidence of the source text and keep its order of argument.
2. Into Japanese: compose each sentence from Japanese exemplars of the same function under [WRITING](../../../runtime/WRITING.md#2-borrow-the-japanese-expression-from-japanese-originals). Sentence-by-sentence rendering of the English wording is replaced by the Japanese exemplars' connectives, endings and rhythm.
3. Into English: take wording from English exemplars of the same function (`exemplars.py find --lang en`).
4. Keep technical terms, equations, quotations, identifiers and numbers exact.
