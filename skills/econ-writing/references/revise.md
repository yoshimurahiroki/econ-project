# Revise economics prose

## Revise

Start from the author's original text and the requested scope. Recover its intended propositions and source-supported relations, then repair information selection, logical order and paragraph connections before surface wording. Reconstruct unclear Japanese as a complete spoken explanation and shorten only after the reader can follow it. Preserve suitable original phrasing and apply the common review to the revised text with the context needed to assess its connections.

Preserve the claim's subject and quantifier through the revision: a weighted population mean, a defined group contrast and a proposition over selected equilibria each retain their mathematical object. Carry a corrected interpretation through the passages that use it even when the saved numbers stay fixed. For multiple referee comments, use the [existing joint revision sequence](paper-workflow.md#appendix-submission-and-referee-response) to repair shared premises once and compose each response from its actual revised location.
