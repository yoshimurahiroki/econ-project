# Revise economics prose

## Revise

1. Recover the author's propositions and keep them: actor, population, quantifier, comparison, uncertainty, quantities, units and attribution.
2. Requests such as 自然な日本語に, 直訳っぽい, わかりやすく短く and 口頭で説明して rewrite the expression: retrieve Japanese exemplars of the same function (`exemplars.py find` and `style`) and rebuild each sentence from their connectives, endings, vocabulary and rhythm under [WRITING](../../../runtime/WRITING.md#2-borrow-the-japanese-expression-from-japanese-originals).
3. Apply the [expression rules](../../../runtime/WRITING.md#3-expression-rules) and keep effective original wording.
4. Referee revisions follow the [joint revision sequence](paper-workflow.md#appendix-submission-and-referee-response).
