# Profile economics prose

## Profile

Create or update a reusable profile when requested. Carry the adopted research question, scientific specification, audience, language and medium into the steps below.

### Select source passages

1. **Output: composition pattern; consumer: example author.** Read the adopted original and retain its version and inspected pages in the existing private source record. Map every paper section to its function, paragraph sequence and length: words for English, characters for Japanese. Record each paragraph's question, opening, evidence and closing inference; identify sentence rhythm, vocabulary and voice. For materials, inspect the rendered page and record canvas size, font family and sizes, line spacing, margins, heading grammar, alignment, exhibit dimensions and reading order. Select the pattern matching research form, language, reader and medium. Before: “Use an academic layout.” After: “Use a nominal heading, a one-sentence result, a full-width comparison table and one sentence linking the comparison to the decision.”

### Derive principles and worked examples

2. **Output: transferable operation and worked pair; consumer: profile composer.** Name the reader's question, the facts establishing its answer and the dependency determining their order. Translate the inspected pattern into one concrete operation beside an original before-and-after pair. Before: “Follow the model paper's style.” After: “Open with the institutional choice, explain its incentive in the next sentence, then give the saved estimate and interval.” Preserve the current study's facts, scientific strength and attribution; keep source-specific wording and private identifiers in their existing records.

### Use a requested opening or comparison

3. **Output: complete original example; consumer: saved profile/template.** Write every section from Abstract through Conclusion at the corresponding model section's scale and level of argument. Give each section its own complete explanation, evidence and inference; retain the model's relative section lengths. For a language conversion, match the argumentative development in natural target-language prose. Before: “Use one paragraph for each chapter.” After: “Develop the model chapter through choices, objective, optimal allocation and its economic interpretation; give each relation its full paragraphs and equations.” Populate the actual template with this independently specified case. Carry the inspected sentence rhythm and paragraph dependencies into its argument. Use a study-specific nominal title and an affirmative opening proposition. Bind numbers, equations and exhibits to the specification and saved results or verified derivations. Use the existing renderer's typography and layouts, with 24 pt slide body text. Keep source correspondence and scientific conditions internally; apply PROSE to the public example.

### Save the profile

4. **Output: inspected profile/material; consumer: writing operation.** Save independently authored principles and complete examples at the existing owner. Keep originals, excerpts, reading logs and source correspondence outside repositories and plugin packages; public citations identify sources. Compare every example section with the corresponding original for length, paragraph function, reasoning, rhythm and academic idiom. Read adjacent sentences together: carry the established actor or economic quantity into the next sentence and state the relation advancing the argument. Before: “企業は800人に対応する。また、費用は2,000円である。したがって、配分を考える。” After: “企業は800人に利用支援を提供できる。支援1件に2,000円を要するため、契約継続による増分利益を基準に支援先を選ぶ。” View both materials at the same display size; compare headings, spacing, exhibit legibility and each page's inference. Repair the producing text or layout and inspect affected outputs. Apply PROSE throughout; preserve third-party notices and attribution.

### Organize selective reading

5. **Output: selective example route; consumer: next composition.** Register stable anchors by research form, passage function, language, reader and medium. Load the principle and complete example resolving the current choice; pass their operation and the current study's facts to composition.
