# Economics writing

1. Recover the requested artifact, audience, language, medium and the settled content. New research content comes from the [worksheet](../../runtime/ECONOMIST.md) (steps 1 to 6); a supplied text keeps its propositions.
2. Choose the mode below and follow it with [WRITING](../../runtime/WRITING.md): argument from English originals, Japanese expression from Japanese exemplars, the expression rules and the templates.
3. Results-based papers, slides and meeting materials use the [result artifacts](references/result-artifacts.md) and [artifact rendering](references/artifact-writing.md) producers.
4. Decision proposals for a firm and pre-analysis plans follow [proposals](references/proposals.md): the worksheet lines each part needs, the order of the document and the values the user sets.
5. Read the rendered output and report its saved paths.

## Registered operations

- [compose](references/compose.md#compose): Compose academic prose and research materials from the adopted sources.
- [revise](references/revise.md#revise): Revise supplied prose, including natural Japanese, spoken explanations and requests such as 自然な日本語に, 何を言いたいの, 直訳っぽい, 口頭で説明して and わかりやすく短く.
- [translate](references/translate.md#translate): Translate supplied prose into natural Japanese or English with source attribution under WRITING.
- [convert](references/convert.md#convert): Convert adopted content into a paper, slides, research site or requested format.
- [guidance](references/guidance.md#guidance): Explain a concrete writing decision and show the needed revision.
- [explain](references/explain.md#explain): Explain a paper, theory, institution, method or result through its question, mechanism, evidence and answer.
- [profile](references/profile.md#profile): Create or revise a reusable writing profile only when explicitly requested.
