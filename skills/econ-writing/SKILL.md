## Compose for the medium and language

Apply [PROSE's audience rule](../../runtime/PROSE.md#select-the-profile) across all seven modes. A local revision or translation uses the selected mode and the passage's surrounding premises and consequences. Read the corresponding [paper guidance](references/paper-workflow.md#compose-the-requested-section) for a requested section or paper architecture, and [paragraph guidance](references/paragraph-workflow.md) for a consequential long-form structural choice. Activate versioned argument and paragraph state when requested source correspondence or persistent dependencies require it. Use profile examples only for a remaining language choice.

Use the following owner for the passage's actual inferential task. Read the selected complete body and its required dependencies; use the adopted specification and saved source/result objects throughout.

| Passage or operation | Canonical procedure |
| --- | --- |
| Empirical interpretation, estimator weights, diagnostics or a direct group contrast | [Identification and empirical method](references/paper-workflow.md#identification-and-empirical-method) |
| Actors, parameters, identification, equilibrium interventions and welfare in a structural study | [Structural estimation and quantitative counterfactuals](references/paper-workflow.md#structural-estimation-and-quantitative-counterfactuals) |
| Proposition, proof, comparative static or welfare derivation in a theory study | [Theory, model and proof](references/paper-workflow.md#theory-model-and-proof) |
| Compose a section from the completed body's supported answer | [Establish the argument](references/paper-workflow.md#establish-the-argument-from-the-research), then the [requested section](references/paper-workflow.md#compose-the-requested-section) |
| Reuse versioned paragraphs or retrieve selected saved evidence | [Paragraph command entry points](references/paragraph-workflow.md#command-entry-points) |
| Bind numerical text, saved definitions, equations or exhibits | [Compose mode](references/compose.md#compose) and the selected [result-artifact contract](references/result-artifacts.md) |

Preserve estimated, calibrated and fixed parameter roles, the actual producing operation, and each quantity's population, timing, units, denominator and scenario. Keep inference and model conditions in the adopted specification and computation; apply PROSE to their expression. Revision, translation and conversion preserve source/result bindings. Each section retains its actual dependencies in the existing stage, so an edit rebuilds its affected consumers and reuses matching completed sections and calculations. Route a determining missing derivation or comparison through econ-design or econ-data and integrate its result within the source and editing authority.

Put the completed wording in the requested artifact. Use [EXECUTION](../../runtime/EXECUTION.md) for saving, rendering, recovery and actual delivery, retaining valid completed content after a failed operation. Complete CORE's integrated review on the finished output.

Select the content by the communication's purpose. A research explanation establishes the finding through its mechanism and identifying comparison. A decision report compares feasible actions on the adopted objective and states the selected action with its determining quantities. A proposal connects the diagnosed obstacle to the result each phase must deliver, its dependencies and the resources required to reach the requested outcome. A progress review starts from the last adopted decision, reports the new result and its implication for the central question, and gives the next executable action. Report a changed hypothesis or priority through the evidence that changed it. Use the existing project state for responsibilities, commitments and outputs.

Before a meeting or review, state the unresolved decision and current hypothesis, then select the investigation and evidence needed to resolve it. Organize discussion around the actual disputed premise, fact or criterion. Explain the relation from an exhibit's comparison to its conclusion in words when the reader needs that intermediate inference. A requested record preserves the adopted decision, its basis, the responsible party, the agreed timing and the remaining dependency through the existing project workflow. Report agreement on an explanation and a change in the substantive judgment through the actual discussion and evidence. Tailor operational updates to the next blocked action, milestone reviews to the accumulated answer and remaining critical dependency, and strategic reviews to objectives and resource allocation. The reader's decision determines detail; the calendar alone supplies no fixed reporting template.

## Output

Return the completed text or artifact first, with its substantive explanation at the requested depth. A rewrite returns the finished wording. Return source/result identities and the actual saved or rendered destination to the same task owner, preserving the claim, quantities, units, audience, medium and editing authority. Report actual changes and decisive evidence when requested. A pending source, calculation, save or render continues through its producing owner under CORE's completion contract.
