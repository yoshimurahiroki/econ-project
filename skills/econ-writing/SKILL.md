## Compose for the medium and language

Apply [PROSE's audience rule](../../runtime/PROSE.md#select-the-profile) across all seven modes. A local revision or translation uses the selected mode and the passage's surrounding premises and consequences. Read the corresponding [paper guidance](references/paper-workflow.md#compose-the-requested-section) for a requested section or paper architecture, and [paragraph guidance](references/paragraph-workflow.md) for a consequential long-form structural choice. Activate versioned argument and paragraph state when requested source correspondence or persistent dependencies require it. Use profile examples only for a remaining language choice.

For requested reuse of versioned body paragraphs, [select their IDs through the existing design command](references/paragraph-workflow.md#command-entry-points). Its architecture and specification callbacks receive the completed prose, supporting claims, prerequisites, adopted exhibits and exact source/result identities with the current brief's research type, reader, language and section function.

For an empirical study, recover the population-level contrast and how the estimator recovers it from the selected sample. Carry observation, assignment, analysis and inference units by their separate roles. Derive the coefficient's interpretation from the adopted equation, weighting and identifying variation; retain its saved contrast, covariance and reference distribution in the specification and result record. A recorded outcome, a causal response and a model-implied response each take their meaning from that generating relation. Use [the empirical writing sequence](references/paper-workflow.md#identification-and-empirical-method) for this inference, then apply PROSE to select the completed passage.

When the requested artifact consumes saved results, bind each result-based section to its quantities and economic definitions through the [existing renderer](../../harness/research.md). Numerical `statistics` bindings retain the adopted unit and format; `definitions` bindings insert the selected comparison, period, equation or other saved text exactly. Build Title, Abstract, Introduction, Results, Discussion and Conclusion from the body's same supported answer and defining conditions, selecting the evidence and explanation needed for each section's function. Revision, translation and conversion preserve those bindings while changing the requested language or presentation. Keep each section's actual dependencies in its existing stage so editing one section preserves the other completed sections and saved calculations.

For a structural study, develop the model's economic argument from the same adopted specification used by estimation. Introduce actors, choices, incentives, information and timing before the equilibrium relation. Explain how observed variation and maintained restrictions identify the named parameters, how likelihood or moments connect the data to those primitives, and how the intervention changes optimized choices and market outcomes. Use the original equations and actual saved estimates to explain the decisive mechanism. Match the text's estimated, calibrated and fixed quantities to their sources, and connect each counterfactual and welfare number to its own scenario, population, units and horizon. Describe an algorithm through the operation it performs and the numerical quantities it produced.

For a pure-theory study, use [the model and proof sequence](references/paper-workflow.md#theory-model-and-proof) to carry the stated primitives through the proposition's domain and quantifiers, its proof or explicit counterexample, and the resulting economic mechanism. Resolve the determining lemma through [the existing theory owner](../econ-design/references/designs.md#propositions-proofs-and-counterexamples). Match a comparative static to its global choice comparison, active constraints and equilibrium selection, and a welfare statement to its evaluated objective and aggregation. The completed derivation supplies the same claim to the Introduction and abstracts in both languages.

Resolve model, identification and welfare conditions through their defining equations and comparisons in the adopted specification and computation. PROSE governs their final expression in every mode. Give the reader the response or quantity that determines the conclusion and derive its economic interpretation. Preserve that explanatory chain when shortening an abstract, paragraph or slide. Keep solver history and continuation details in the existing execution/result record; the research text presents the adopted model and computed economic results.

Route a determining missing derivation or comparison through econ-design or econ-data and integrate its result. Preserve source-bound revision and translation within the source and editing scope.

Put the completed wording in the requested artifact. Recover a failed save, render or registration through EXECUTION's existing owner, retaining completed content. Inspect the actual saved or rendered output and report its substantive result and destination. These operations complete one task with its integrated review; the requested output determines any intermediate transcript or review document.

Select the content by the communication's purpose. A research explanation establishes the finding through its mechanism and identifying comparison. A decision report compares feasible actions on the adopted objective and states the selected action with its determining quantities. A proposal connects the diagnosed obstacle to the result each phase must deliver, its dependencies and the resources required to reach the requested outcome. A progress review starts from the last adopted decision, reports the new result and its implication for the central question, and gives the next executable action. Report a changed hypothesis or priority through the evidence that changed it. Use the existing project state for responsibilities, commitments and outputs.

Before a meeting or review, state the unresolved decision and current hypothesis, then select the investigation and evidence needed to resolve it. Organize discussion around the actual disputed premise, fact or criterion. Explain the relation from an exhibit's comparison to its conclusion in words when the reader needs that intermediate inference. A requested record preserves the adopted decision, its basis, the responsible party, the agreed timing and the remaining dependency through the existing project workflow. Report agreement on an explanation and a change in the substantive judgment through the actual discussion and evidence. Tailor operational updates to the next blocked action, milestone reviews to the accumulated answer and remaining critical dependency, and strategic reviews to objectives and resource allocation. The reader's decision determines detail; the calendar alone supplies no fixed reporting template.

## Output

Return the completed text or artifact first, with the substantive explanation the request calls for. When the task is a rewrite, deliver the finished wording. Report actual changes and decisive evidence when a completion report is requested.

Return the completed mode's text, source/result identities and actual saved or rendered output to the same task owner. Preserve the adopted claim, quantities, units, audience, medium and editing authority across that return. When a source, calculation, save or render remains a prerequisite, keep that operation open through its producing owner and retain the valid completed wording; the requested artifact and authorized actions determine completion.
