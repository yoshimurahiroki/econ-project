## Finite-state dynamic game estimation

The existing finite-state logit game accepts `estimation_method: "nfxp"` (default) or `"mpec"`. NFXP maximizes the action likelihood after solving the policy equilibrium at each payoff parameter. MPEC jointly chooses free payoff parameters and policy log odds subject to those same best-response equations; value functions are concentrated through `(I - beta P_policy) V = expected_flow + shock_scale * entropy`. Fixed parameter values, the supplied action-profile transition kernels and the adopted equilibrium reached from `initial_policy_logits` remain part of the model. Save optimizer termination, policy and Bellman residuals, the local observed-state choice-response rank and the re-solved likelihood. Use `counterfactual.profiles` to change the adopted payoffs, feasible profiles or transition law and solve all firms again at the estimated parameters. Keep a Rust replacement model's reset rule and data transition estimator in its matching engine, and a continuous-time game's generator in its continuous-time engine.
